## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Started by | R4 or R5 |
| Needs | 10+ members at Sanctuary 6 or higher |
| Attempts | 3 per player (per day by most guides, per event by the wiki) |
| Structure | 6 camps opened in sequence, each with a fixed HP pool |
| Pays | Skill Badges, Alliance Coins, Study Scrolls, speedups, Curio Chests |

## The camps

| Camp | Monsters | Clear chest |
|---|---|---|
| Basic Shack | 40 | 500 badges, 2,500 coins |
| Basic Camp | 40 | 1,000 badges, 5,000 coins |
| Intermediate Camp | 50 | 1,000 badges, 5,000 coins |
| Intermediate Fortress | 50 | 1,500 badges, 7,500 coins |
| Advanced Camp | 60 | 2,000 badges, 10,000 coins |
| Boss Fortress | boss | no chest, but counts for rankings |

## Damage stacking

Your cumulative damage over the event grants a lasting bonus: 2 to 5M +2%, 5 to 10M +4%, 10 to 16M +6%, 16 to 25M +8%, 25M and up +10%. Early attempts make later attempts hit harder.

## Four reward pools

1. **Alliance progress ranking** (top 20 alliances on the server by damage): speedup bundles for everyone.
2. **Server personal damage ranking**: UR Curio Chests at the top, SSR supplies down to rank 500.
3. **Inside-alliance damage ranking**: Study Scrolls 200 / 100 / 50 / 20 for the top ten.
4. **Camp clear chests** as above.

## KMA plan

> **KMA:** Put all three attempts on the **highest open camp**. Strong hitters open a camp, weaker members finish it, so nobody's attempt lands on a camp that is already dead.

Officers pick a window when most are online, clear camps in order to open the next tier early, and push for a top-20 server rank so the shared speedup pool pays.

## Mistakes

- Spending an attempt on a low camp that others will clear anyway.
- Hitting a camp at 1% HP with your strongest squad.
- Not bringing your monster-damage heroes (Stellar, Grenwald, Bella auras).
