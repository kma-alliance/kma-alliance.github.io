## Personal challenge

The Hell Nurse ladder: 9 difficulties of 30 encounters. Rally if stuck. Your personal Nurse level is what gates the alliance bosses below, so push it early in the window.

## Alliance challenge

- R4 or R5 summon bosses **one at a time in strict order**.
- Each boss lasts **30 minutes** with **one retry**. A second failure closes that boss and every one after it.
- The member list locks at event start. Only fighters get the reward, paid once per boss.

| Boss | Needs |
|---|---|
| 1 | Open |
| 2 | 8 players at Nurse level 2+ |
| 3 | 10 at level 3 |
| 4 | 15 at level 4 |
| 5 | 20 at level 5 |
| 6 | 25 at level 6 |
| 7 | 30 at level 7 |
| 8 | 35 at level 8 |
| 9 | 40 at level 9 |

Rewards include UR hero shards, Raven Essence and diamonds.

## Tips

- SSR gauntlets beat UR for monster damage in this mode.
- Scouting a city before you attack triggers War Frenzy, so do it early.

> **KMA:** Officers will announce which boss is being summoned and when. Do not be mid-march elsewhere; a failed retry closes the rest of the ladder for everyone.
