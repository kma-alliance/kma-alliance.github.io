## Alliance Boss

| | |
|---|---|
| Levels | 12. Level 3 opens on server day 7, level 4 on day 14, then one a week |
| Faction bonus | Does not apply |
| Reward slots | Any hit 1, 2M damage 2, 4M 3, 6M 4, 8M 5, 10M 6, one more slot per 2M |

Any damage at all earns a reward slot, so hit it every time it is up. Join the officer rally rather than starting your own.

## Alliance Digs

Dig speed scales with the **square** of the number of diggers.

| Diggers | Time |
|---|---|
| 1 | 2 hours |
| 2 | 30 minutes |
| 5 | under 5 minutes |
| 10 | 72 seconds |
| 20 | 18 seconds |
| 100 | 1 second |

Up to 100 people per dig, up to 10 gifts per spot, 24 hours to collect. 37 spot types.

> **KMA:** Never dig alone. Post the dig in chat, wait for people to pile in, and everyone collects.

## Mistakes

- Starting a dig quietly and sitting on it for two hours.
- Forgetting the Alliance Boss because it did not have a timer in mail.
