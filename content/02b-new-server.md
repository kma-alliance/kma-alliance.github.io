A new server moves fast. Some things are gated by your **Sanctuary level**, which you control, and some by **server day**, which nobody controls. This page is about the second kind: what the calendar hands you and when, so the alliance is ready a week early instead of a day late.

## Your server, right now

Enter your server day once on [Your account](#/my-account) and the timeline below fills in real dates for **your** server, with what is coming in the next two weeks at the top.

<div data-widget="server-timeline"></div>

> **Note:** read your server day from the **Monument** building. It counts the day the server opened as **day 1**. Some guide sites count from 0, so their hero days run one lower than the game. Player stream titles like "Era of Revival Day 24" are **Era days**, not server days.

## Week by week

### Days 1 to 7: build fast, choose well

| Day | What opens |
|---|---|
| 1 | Marlena and Arthur. Quiz of Wisdom, Final Dawn and Wandering Phantom open by Sanctuary level |
| 2 | Survival Battle (Sanctuary 7) |
| 3 | Demon King Blight |
| 4 | Cheese Trap (trap levels 1 and 2), Caravans, Top Healer (7 days) |
| 7 | Cheese Trap level 3 |

This is the week alliances form and the strongest players pull ahead. Players on one new server reached Sanctuary 20 by about day 5 or 6, so do not assume there is time to settle in.

### Days 8 to 14: AvA starts

- **Day 8:** **Cynthia** arrives on a hero wheel with free daily spins, and **Shadow** in a paid hero pass.
- **First Monday on or after day 8:** the first **Alliance Duel** week, the event players call **AvA**. On one new server the top alliance's first opponent was the server's number-two alliance. There is no raid day yet. See [AvA](#/ava).
- **Days 12 to 18:** the first Crystal Cluster Valley session (Sanctuary 15).
- **First Friday from about day 13:** the first **Elixir Scramble**. Register Monday to Wednesday. It needs Sanctuary 15 and an alliance at least a week old.
- **Day 14:** Cheese Trap level 4.

### Days 15 to 28: the alliance events arrive

| Day | What opens |
|---|---|
| 15 | **Undead Siege** (the official update notes say two weeks after server launch). **Joker** on the hero wheel, **Annie** in the hero pass |
| 15 to 21 | **Canyon Conquest** first round: 15 members at Sanctuary 15+, three squads each |
| About 20 | First **Thief Hunt**, then roughly every two weeks |
| 21 | Cheese Trap level 5 |
| 22 | **Harper** in a hero pass |
| 27 | First **Royal City Scramble** |

The alliance-level cities and the throne are usually settled by diplomacy in weeks one to three, before the first Royal City Scramble on day 27. That is the time to talk to the other top alliances, not after.

### Days 29 to 60: the server goes to war

| Day | What changes |
|---|---|
| 29 | **Kingdom War announced.** Alliance Duels go cross-server with a Saturday raid. Cross-server caravans. **Daskal** in a hero pass |
| 30 | Cheese Trap level 6 |
| 31 | **Harper** joins the Tavern recruit pool |
| About 35 to 48 | First **Kingdom War** battles, on a Saturday. See [Kingdom War](#/kingdom-war) |
| 36 | **Red Lady** |
| 43 | **Ulfrid** (formerly Brian) |
| 45 | Cheese Trap level 7 |
| 50+ | Alliance League eligibility |
| 57 | **Zoya** |
| 60 | Cheese Trap level 8 |

After that: Louis on day 64, Bell on 71, Raven Runes on 78, Billy on 85, Nicole on 99, Cheese Trap level 10 on 120, and the **Era of Revival** at roughly day 136 to 160, when servers enter in groups of 32 and close to new characters.

## The KMA plan for a new server

> **KMA:** Put the whole alliance's weight behind the first **AvA** week and the first **Royal City Scramble**. Early wins there decide which alliance holds the cities and the throne for months, and the throne's court posts give real growth buffs to whoever holds them.

> **Unverified:** a few dates are estimates. The first Kingdom War battle date varies by server, the first Elixir Scramble can fall a week later than shown, and one older player list puts the first Alliance Duel on day 18. Check the in-game event icons, and tell an officer if your server differs so this page can be corrected.

## Quick answers

**What is AvA?** Players' name for the **Alliance Duel**, alliance versus alliance. It starts on the first Monday on or after day 8. Full guide: [AvA](#/ava).

**When is KvK?** Kingdom War is announced on **day 29**. The first battles usually come on a Saturday somewhere between days 35 and 48. Full guide: [Kingdom War](#/kingdom-war).

**Where do I find my server day?** In the **Monument** building. Enter it on [Your account](#/my-account).

**When does the Era of Revival start?** Around day 136 to 160. Servers enter in groups of 32, announced five days ahead.
