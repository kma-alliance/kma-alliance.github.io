## Why server age matters more than you think

Most of this game is gated by **Sanctuary level**, which you control. A short list of things is gated by **server day**, which nobody controls, and those are the ones worth planning around:

- **Cheese Trap levels** step up on a fixed calendar, so the alliance's damage ceiling rises whether or not anyone is ready.
- **Daily Offer heroes** appear on set days. Harper on day 22, Daskal on day 29, Red Lady on 36, Zoya on 57, Louis on 64, Bell on 71, Billy on 85, Nicole on 99. If you want one, bank for the day it appears.
- **Royal City Scramble** first runs on day 27 and **Kingdom War** is announced on day 29. That pair turns the server from a growth race into a war server.
- **Eras** roll every 56 days, each with its own Era Pass.
- Around **day 130** the server enters the Era of Revival and closes to new characters.
