## Why server age matters more than you think

Most of this game is gated by **Sanctuary level**, which you control. A short list of things is gated by **server day**, which nobody controls, and those are the ones worth planning around:

- **AvA** (the Alliance Duel) starts on the first Monday on or after day 8, on your own server with no raid day. It goes cross-server around day 29. See [AvA](#/ava).
- **Royal City Scramble** first runs on day 27, and **Kingdom War** is announced on day 29. The first war battles usually come on a Saturday between days 35 and 48. See [Kingdom War](#/kingdom-war).
- **Heroes** arrive on set days: Cynthia and Shadow on day 8, Joker and Annie on 15, Harper on 22, Daskal on 29, Red Lady on 36, Ulfrid on 43, Zoya on 57, Louis on 64, Bell on 71, Billy on 85, Nicole on 99.
- **Cheese Trap levels** step up on a fixed calendar, so the alliance's damage ceiling rises whether or not anyone is ready.
- Around **day 136 to 160** the server enters the Era of Revival, in a group of 32 servers, and closes to new characters.

Read your server day from the **Monument** building, which counts the opening day as day 1. Entries marked **likely** have good evidence but vary a little by server; entries marked **estimate** are rough.
