## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Needs | Sanctuary 8 |
| When | Every Wednesday and Saturday |
| Attacks | 10 per day, solo, **no rallies** |
| Variants | Warrior boss (bring Rangers), Ranger boss (bring Warlocks), Warlock boss (bring Warriors) |

## Rewards

Daily achievements: 1 attack pays 2 SR Herb supplies, 10 diamonds and 200 gearstones; 2 attacks add 3 herbs, 10 diamonds and 200 gearstones; 3 attacks add 5 herbs, 3× 10 diamonds and 600 gearstones. **Do at least three.**

Server damage ranking: rank 1 gets 2,800 gearstones, 12 SR Herb supplies and 40 Tempered Steel; ranks 4 to 10 get 1,500, 8 and 18.

In Kingdom War weeks, the kingdom with the highest total damage earns 250,000 Kingdom Points and the personal #1 earns 150,000, which is why every member's ten attacks matter.

## KMA plan

> **KMA:** Ten attacks each on Wednesday and Saturday with the counter faction. If you only have a Warrior squad, it still counts; just expect less damage against the Warrior boss.
