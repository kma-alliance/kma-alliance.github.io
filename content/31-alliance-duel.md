## How it works

Two alliances are matched for six days, **Monday to Saturday**, one theme per day. Days reset at **00:00 UTC**. Each day is scored separately, so an item spent on the wrong day scores nothing.

| Day | Theme | In-game name |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | Raven | Enhance Raven |
| Tuesday | Construction | Territory Development |
| Wednesday | Tech | Tech Research |
| Thursday | Heroes | Hero Growth |
| Friday | Preparation | War Preparation |
| Saturday | Raid | Raid Enemy |

Rewards: daily milestone chests, daily ranking, daily victory, and weekly alliance victory or defeat rewards. The weekly Study Scroll payout is **840 for a win and 420 for a loss**. A Duel win is also worth 30,000 Kingdom Points in Kingdom War, and the MVP gets 6,000.

Survival Battle runs in parallel with its own rotating themes. When its theme matches the Duel day, the same burn scores twice.

> **KMA:** Officers post the weekly save plan in alliance mail on Sunday. If you only read one thing in this handbook, read the cheat sheet below.

|---|---|---|
| Raven Essence, Raven Fruit, Stamina | **Day 1 only** | Only day 1 scores them |
| Falcon Quests (finish them, do not claim) | Days 1, 3, 5 | 10,000 each on claim. A quest with a red dot never expires |
| Caravan Tickets, Black Ops Tickets, Survivor Tickets | Day 2 (caravan and ops also score day 6) | 100,000 and 75,000 flat bombs |
| Construction speedups, finished builds waiting for the hammer | Day 2 (spill to 5 and 6) | 50 per minute |

| Hero shards, Recruit Tickets, Antitoxin, Skill Badges | **Day 4 only** | Highest per-item values |
| Training boosts, a stockpile of low-tier troops to promote | Day 5 | Promotion ladder scoring |
| Healing speedups, leftover speedups, shields | Day 6 | Heal after fights. Shield if you are not a fighter |

**Points register when you tap the completion hammer, not when the timer ends.** Finish builds and research before the day, tap after 00:00 UTC.

## Day by day

Base values below are from lastasylumplague.com. Your in-game numbers scale with your Alliance Duel research; alliances with Arena Expert see roughly double.

### Day 1: Raven (Monday)

| Action | Points |
|---|---|
| Claim a Falcon Quest | 10,000 |
| Use 1 Raven Essence | 2,500 |
| Use 1 Stamina | 150 |
| Use 1 Raven Fruit | 1 to 3 |
| Gather 100 grain, timber or herbs | 5 |

Prep on Sunday: send gathering squads so tiles finish Monday, pre-complete Falcon Quests and hold them. Rallies and Raven upgrades also count.

### Day 2: Construction (Tuesday)

| Action | Points |
|---|---|
| Dispatch a UR Caravan | 100,000 |
| Run a UR Covert Operation | 75,000 |
| Recruit a survivor | 1,500 |
| 1 minute of construction speedup | 50 |
| +1 Building Might | 10 |

Queue long builds on Monday. Tap the hammer after reset.

### Day 3: Tech (Wednesday)

| Level 5 chest | 90,000 |

Start long research on Tuesday so it lands on Wednesday. Hoard every Raven Gear Chest all week. Demon King also runs on Wednesday.

| Action | Points |

Promote troops **one tier at a time**. Each step scores as training the new tier.

### Day 6: Raid (Saturday)

| Action | Points |
|---|---|
| Dispatch a UR Caravan | 100,000 |
| Run a UR Covert Operation | 75,000 |
| Kill a T10 soldier belonging to the **matched** alliance | 55 (10 to 55 by tier) |

| 1 minute of any speedup, including healing | 50 |

Kills against the matched alliance pay about five times a random kill. Losses also score, so fighting is never wasted, but dead T10s cost more than they earn.

> **KMA:** On Saturday the God of War posts targets inside the matched alliance. Fighters hit those. Everyone else shields, heals, and burns the rest of their speedups. Do not free-fight.

## What to avoid

- Spending Essence, Fruit, shards or chests on the wrong day. Zero points.
- Tapping hammers before the day opens.
- Claiming Falcon Quests as they come in.
- Buying diamond packs "for points". It is 30 points, once.
- Opening Raven chests before Wednesday.
- Getting farmed on Saturday without a shield.
- Skipping the research tree. Unresearched alliances score about half. See [Research](#/research).

## For officers

- Post the save plan on Sunday. Remind at each reset.
- Track daily milestone chest claims. Aim for 7 to 8 reward tiers a day.
- Check members have the Lab level for the Duel tree and T7 to T9 access.
- Line up Survival Battle themes so members double-dip.
- Saturday: pre-assign targets in the matched alliance, organise rallies, tell weaker members to shield.
