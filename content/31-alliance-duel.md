Alliance Duel is the event the whole week is built around. Every day has one theme, and only that theme's actions score. So the Duel is not won by spending the most. It is won by **spending the right items on the right day**, and by every member clearing their daily milestones.

## How it works

Alliances are matched for six days, **Monday to Saturday**. Before your kingdom qualifies for the League it is one alliance against one other; once it qualifies, **six alliances share a weekly group** (see below). Days roll at **00:00 server time**, not midnight UTC and not your midnight. Check the [Timezones](#/time-zones) page for what that is on your clock. Each day is scored separately, so an item spent on the wrong day scores nothing.

Survival Battle runs in parallel with its own rotating themes. When its theme matches the Duel day, the same burn scores twice. See [Survival Battle](#/survival-battle).

## The League

The official Facebook page announced the League on 11 June 2026. It sits on top of the Duel and does **not** add a seventh day or change the six themes.

Weekly results move alliances up to higher divisions. Everyone taking part gets Study Scroll, Raven Gear Chest, Gearstone and more. In-game, the League panel sits inside the Duel screen and shows your group standing.

> **Unverified:** guide sites name the divisions Elite, Epic and Mythic, but that comes from a site that invents English names. Check the League screen. Payout details are on the [Alliance League](#/alliance-league) page.

## Rewards

| Reward | What it takes |
|---|---|
| Daily milestone chests | Points on the day. **9 chests**; tiers 4 to 6 need the Super Rewards research, tiers 7 to 9 need Best Rewards |
| Daily ranking | Your place on the day's leaderboard |
| Daily victory | Your alliance wins the day |
| Weekly victory or defeat | **840** Study Scrolls for a win, **420** for a loss |
| Kingdom War | A Duel win is worth **30,000** Kingdom Points; the MVP gets **6,000** |

Two players' day-by-day videos show about **7 million points** to open the last daily chest on their accounts. Your target will differ with your level, but it shows how much a full day asks for.

> **KMA:** The recommended rhythm is a save plan in alliance mail on Sunday and a reminder at each reset. If you only read one thing in this handbook, read the cheat sheet below.

## The cheat sheet

The [Duel planner](#/duel-planner) scores your actual banked items against every day.

<div data-widget="duel-week"></div>

| Save these | Spend on | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Raven Essence, Raven Fruit, Stamina, Epigraph Chests | **Day 1** | Day 1 is the Raven day |
| Falcon Quests (finish them, do not claim) | Days 1, 3, 5 | 10,000 each on claim. A quest with a red dot never expires |
| Caravan and Covert Operation refresh items, Survivor Recruit Tickets | Day 2 (caravans and ops also score day 6) | 100,000 and 75,000 flat bombs |
| Construction speedups, finished builds waiting for the hammer | Day 2 (spill to 5 and 6) | 50 per minute |
| Raven Gear Chests, Study Scrolls, research speedups | **Day 3 only** | Chests only score on day 3 |
| Hero shards, Recruit Tickets, Antitoxin, Skill Badges | **Day 4** | Highest per-item values |
| Training boosts, a stockpile of low-tier troops to promote | Day 5 | Training scores by tier |
| Healing speedups, leftover speedups, shields | Day 6 | Heal after fights. Shield if you are not a fighter |

**Points register when you tap the completion hammer, not when the timer ends.** Finish builds and research before the day, then tap after the 00:00 server-time reset.

> **Tip:** the Falcon Tower holds a limited number of finished quests: player videos show **35** on one account and **40** on a later one. Bank to the cap the day before days 1, 3 and 5, then claim after reset.

## The KMA plan

> **KMA:** Saturday is the fighting day. The recommended plan: the God of War picks targets inside the matched alliance, fighters hit those, and everyone else shields, heals and burns their remaining speedups. Do not free-fight.

- **Sunday:** bank Falcon Quests to the cap, queue long builds, send gatherers so they land after reset.
- **Monday to Friday:** clear your daily milestones with the items saved for that day, and nothing else.
- **Saturday:** shield up right after reset unless you are fighting. Every soldier you lose feeds the other side's score.

## For officers

- Post the save plan on Sunday. Remind at each reset.
- Track daily milestone chest claims. Aim for 7 to 8 reward tiers a day.
- Check members have the Lab level for the Duel tree and T7 to T9 access.
- Line up Survival Battle themes so members double-dip.
- Saturday: pre-assign targets in the matched alliance, organise rallies, tell weaker members to shield.

## Quick answers

**When does a Duel day start on my clock?** At 00:00 server time, which is UTC-2. The countdown at the top of this page shows the time left.

**I finished a build at 23:50. Did it score?** Not yet. Points register when you tap the hammer. Wait until after reset and tap it then.

**Do Raven Gear Chests score on any other day?** No. Only day 3 counts them, so hold them all week.

**I am weak. Should I fight on Saturday?** No. Shield, heal and spend leftover speedups. Your losses would score points for the other side.

**Does the League change what I do each day?** No. The six days stay the same. The League changes who you are grouped with and what the week pays.
