## How to use it on a Sunday

1. Open your bag and count what you actually have: Falcon Quests sitting unclaimed, Raven Essence, Caravan and Black Ops tickets, speedup minutes, hero shards.
2. Type them in. The day with the biggest bar is the day worth planning your week around.
3. Anything scoring zero on its day is an item you are wasting. Raven Essence spent on a Tuesday is worth nothing at all.

The daily score reward lands at **180,000 points** and the weekly one at **550,000**. Those are the two lines the tool checks against.

## The single biggest multiplier

The **Arena Expert** node in the Alliance Duel research tree costs 3,800 Study Scrolls and doubles every point source in the event. Buy Super Rewards (100 scrolls) and Best Rewards (450) first to unlock the higher chest tiers, then Arena Expert. An alliance without that research scores roughly half what a researched one does from identical play. See [Research priorities](#/research).
