## Rules

- **Officers lead.** Rallies led by the R5 or a title holder get **+5% damage**, R4-led rallies +2.5%. A member-led rally gets nothing and splits the alliance.
- **Join with your best squad.** Rally contribution scales with troops sent, so capacity matters: Training Grounds level, Load Training research, VIP, hero leadership and event buffs.
- **Squad 1 is your rally squad.** Highest tier troops, faction-matched heroes, max leadership.
- Counter-pick when you can. Bring the faction that beats the defender's dominant faction for the 20% swing. See [Hero tier list](#/hero-tier-list).

## The one-hero trick

Officers start rallies with a **single hero with the lowest troop load**. The leader burns almost no troops, everyone still gets the leader bonus, and the members' squads do the work.

## Auto-rally and offline join

Set auto-rally to **officer rallies only**. When you are offline, your strongest squad joins 20 seconds before the rally closes if a slot is free.

> **Warning:** Turn auto-rally **off** before Undead Siege and defense nights so your garrison stays home.

## March times

Event Blights in Hunt Battle use a fixed 15-second march each way. Normal world-map marches scale with distance, reduced by alliance War Research (rally speed) and territory bonuses. Live near the rally leaders.

Cheese Trap rallies are 3 minutes each. With two lineups you can keep one marching while the other queues, which fits 45 to 50 rallies into the 30-minute window.

## What you can rally

| Target | Notes |
|---|---|
| Elite and Wandering Blights | World map PvE. Wandering Phantom from Sanctuary 8. Rally rewards for the organiser run from 16,851 at level 1 to 616,794 at level 30; participants 3,000 to 64,000. |
| Cheese Thief | Cheese Trap. 20 members, Mouse Lure buff. |
| Alliance Boss | Any hit gives a pull. Faction bonus does not apply. |
| Thief Leader | Thief Hunt. No stamina cost to join. 78 Lion Coins, 20 diamonds, 1 Recruit Ticket per rally. |
| Cities and fortresses | You can only attack a city adjacent to one you already hold. Take the perimeter turrets first or they shred your garrison. |
| Player territories | Saturday Duel targets and Kingdom War. Assigned by the God of War only. |

Demon King is **not** rallyable: 10 solo attacks a day.

## Energy

Solo targets cost 10 energy, rallies 20. Energy refills 1 every 6 minutes to a cap of 120. Joining Thief Leader rallies is free.
