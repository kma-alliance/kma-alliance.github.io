## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| First | Server day 27, then weekly |
| Window | 8 hours |
| Needs | Sanctuary 15 to enter; alliance must hold a level 6 city (relaxed in the Era of Revival) |
| Location | Cursed land in the centre of the continent. No shields, no Falcon Tower, boosted march speed |
| Losses | 70% of fallen troops go to the infirmary instead of dying |

## Winning

First alliance to hold the city for **4 continuous hours** wins; 8 hours unbroken wins instantly; otherwise longest total occupation. (One guide says most total time is the whole rule. Verify on your server.) Enemy-held turrets fire on the city, so take the turrets first. Reinforcements stack onto a structure your alliance already holds.

The winning R5 becomes **King**, gets the Temple Battle court, and mails supply chests to participants.

## Honor Points for everyone else

Attack the red-hammer resource tiles in the mud ring around the city, or park troops and let enemies hit you.

| Honor | Reward |
|---|---|
| 10,000 | 2,500 Alliance Coins, 1,800 Fame Medals, speedups |
| 20,000 | Coins, 80 general supplies |
| 40,000 | Coins, 3,100 Fame Medals |
| 70,000 | Coins, 220 supplies |
| 100,000 | 2× 2,500 Coins, 5,100 Fame Medals, 55 speedups |

Heal 200 to 300 troops at a time to save speedups. **Stop at 100,000.** Nothing beyond that pays.
