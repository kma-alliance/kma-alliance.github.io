## Alliance Boss

| | |
|---|---|
| Levels | 12. Level 3 opens on server day 7, level 4 on day 14, then one a week |
| Faction bonus | Does not apply |
| Reward slots | Any hit 1, 2M damage 2, 4M 3, 6M 4, 8M 5, 10M 6, one more slot per 2M |

Any damage at all earns a reward slot, so hit it every time it is up. Join the officer rally rather than starting your own.

## Alliance Excavation

last-asylum.com, which presents itself as the official site, calls this **Alliance Excavation**, under Falcon Quests. Many players call it **Alliance Expedition** instead, and older guides on other sites call it "digs" or "dig spots". All three names describe the same thing: a shared site on the alliance map that runs faster the more members tap it.

Speed scales with the **square** of the number of helpers.

| People on the spot | Time |
|---|---|
| 1 | 2 hours |
| 2 | 30 minutes |
| 5 | under 5 minutes |
| 10 | 72 seconds |
| 20 | 18 seconds |
| 100 | 1 second |

Reported limits: up to 100 people per spot, up to 10 gifts, 24 hours to collect, 37 spot types.

> **KMA:** If this does exist on our server, the rule is simple: never start one alone. Post it in chat and wait for people to pile in, because the whole point is that each extra person speeds it up for everyone.

## Mistakes

- Starting one quietly and sitting on it for two hours.
- Forgetting the Alliance Boss because it did not have a timer in mail.
