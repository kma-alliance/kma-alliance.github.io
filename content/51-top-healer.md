## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Needs | Sanctuary 6 |
| Format | 7 contests, one per day, each counting only its own action set |
| Universal | Diamond pack purchases 30 points per diamond in every contest. UR covert op 2,000. UR trade caravan 5,000 |

## The seven contests

| # | Contest | Big action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Resource Acquisition | Falcon Quest 1,000 |
| 2 | Construction | Recruit survivor 400 |
| 3 | Technology Research | Falcon Quest 1,000 |
| 4 | Hero Recruitment | Recruit hero 400 |
| 5 | Soldier Training | Nothing big; speedups at 20 per minute |
| 6 | Full Development | UR Trade Caravan 5,000 |
| 7 | Survival Battle | UR Trade Caravan 5,000 |

A variant swaps contest 1 for Raven Boost (Falcon Quest 1,350, Raven Essence 300) with thresholds up to 180,000 points.

## Thresholds

| Points | Reward |
|---|---|
| 500 | 2× SR Level Supply of each resource |
| 1,000 | 2× SR Level Supply of each |
| 3,000 | 6× SR Level Supply of each |
| 6,000 | 2 SSR Hero Omni Shards, 3× 60m speedup, 2 Recruit Tickets |
| 10,000 | 1 UR Hero Omni Shard, 3 SR Antitoxin supplies, 2 Recruit Tickets |

## Plan

Finish Falcon Quests but claim them on contests 1 and 3. Hold UR caravans and covert ops for days 6 and 7. Save survivor and hero recruits for days 2 and 4. Dump training speedups on day 5 since nothing else scores there. It does **not** score like the Alliance Duel; each contest only counts its own actions.
