A good daily routine is not about doing everything. It is about never letting something fill up and stop paying. Idle income stops after 8 hours, energy stops at 120, and an unfinished Falcon Quest vanishes after 8 hours. Other things, like a missed Expedition window or a skipped Covert Op, are simply gone. The groups below follow that order.

Your ticks are stored in this browser only. They do not reset on their own, so untick at the start of your day.

## Every login

- [ ] **Collect idle income** before it hits the 8-hour cap, and tap production buildings. The Antitoxin Workshop holds 8 to 12 hours depending on its level.
- [ ] **Refill every queue**: all build queues and both research queues (the second comes from the 2nd Workbench at Sanctuary 9).
- [ ] **Alliance help**: request help on every build, research and heal, and tap the help icon for others. The first 100 helps you give each day pay rewards.
- [ ] **Falcon Quests**: take new ones. Half of them cost no energy. Complete them before they expire. On Duel days 2 and 4, and on Sunday, leave finished quests **unclaimed** for the next scoring day.
- [ ] **Energy**: spend it before the bar fills. Plague Rat Hordes drop Recruit Tickets; Grain, Timber and Herb Blights drop Raven Fruit. During Thief Hunt, Gold Thieves give Lion Coins and the most Raven Fruit per hit.
- [ ] **Check alliance mail and chat** for event times and rally calls.

## Once a day, early in your day

- [ ] **Alliance Duel** (Monday to Saturday): spend the items that score for today's theme. See [Alliance Duel](#/alliance-duel).
- [ ] **Claim Falcon Quests** on Duel days 1, 3 and 5. Each one is worth 10,000 points on those days.
- [ ] **Free pulls**: one free call in Surprise Encounter, the daily free Wish Coin on the Wishing Wheel, and the free pull in each recruit pool whenever its own timer is ready.
- [ ] **Free Stamina**: claim both free Stamina pickups; the second opens 4 hours after the first.
- [ ] **Arena**: your free battles. The lowest arena gives 5 a day, worth up to 250,000 Antitoxin (50,000 a win, 25,000 a loss).
- [ ] **Covert Operations**: send the most expensive ops your heroes qualify for, then do your 5 assists and 5 intercepts on the most expensive ops you can see. Each pays a tenth of the main reward.

## Once a day, any time

- [ ] **Expedition** (Sanctuary 16): push while a window is open. Windows open Sunday, Tuesday and Thursday and last 48 hours; a missed window is gone.
- [ ] **Daily and chapter quests**: fill the activity bar to 100 and open the chest.
- [ ] **Alliance contribution**: donate to alliance tech. Diamond contributions get dearer each time (2 diamonds for the first, 50 for the 25th, 650 for all 25), so the first few are the good ones.
- [ ] **Alliance events with attempts**: Alliance Expedition (3 attempts; save them for the highest open camp), Alliance Boss (any hit gives a reward pull), and Hunt Battle when an officer calls it.
- [ ] **After Rat Swarm waves**: repair towers and restock medicine.

## Before you log off

- [ ] **Queue the longest jobs** you can afford in every build and research queue.
- [ ] **War week?** Do your last scout, rally or reinforcement at least 15 minutes before you shield (War Frenzy), then shield.
- [ ] **Defense night?** Garrison set, and auto-join rallies turned off.

- [ ] **Quiz of Wisdom (Sundays, Sanctuary 6+)**: 15 questions, 10 diamonds per right answer and 5 per wrong one, plus milestone rewards at 1, 3, 5, 7 and 10 correct. See [Quiz of Wisdom](#/quiz).
- [ ] **Shields**: buy with Alliance Coins before a war week, not with diamonds.
- [ ] **Alliance Shop**: check it after the weekly refresh for UR Omni Shards and Raven Essence. The Monday refresh is reported by the publisher's guide but not independently confirmed.
- [ ] **Plan the Duel week**: look at the **Weekly rhythm** on the home page. On Sunday, finish Falcon Quests and hold the claims for Monday.

## Never

- [ ] Do not open Level Supplies early. They pay by your level **when opened**: Antitoxin supplies pay up to 57 times more late in the game than at level 1, resource supplies about 3 times more.
- [ ] Do not scout, reinforce or attack within 15 minutes of when you need to shield. Any military action locks shielding for 15 minutes (War Frenzy).
- [ ] Do not buy extra arena battles. The price climbs every time (100, 200, 300, 400, then 500 diamonds) and the reward does not.

## Quick answers

**When does my day reset?** At 00:00 server time, which is UTC-2. Find your local time on [Time zones](#/time-zones).

**Should I hold Falcon Quests for days?** No. Storage fills in under a day, then every refresh is wasted. Hold for one day at most: finish on Duel days 2 and 4 (and Sunday), claim on days 1, 3 and 5.

**I only have 10 minutes. What matters most?** Collect idle income, refill queues, spend energy, and send Covert Ops. Those are the ones that overflow or cannot be made up.
