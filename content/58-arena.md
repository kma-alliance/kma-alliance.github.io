The Arena is the cheapest steady income in the game. The building costs almost nothing, has one level, and never blocks your build queue. After that, every day pays Antitoxin just for pressing fight, and every season pays diamonds for your rank. What decides success is simple: **take all five free fights every day**, and pick opponents you can beat so more of them are wins.

## How it works

You fight other players' saved defense squads. Nobody is online on the other side, so it is your squad's Power, faction and skills against theirs. In the lowest arena **a win pays 50,000 Antitoxin and a loss still pays 25,000**. Even five losses are worth 125,000 Antitoxin, so there is never a reason to skip.

### There is more than one arena

The game has several arenas. The lowest opens almost at once; the higher ones need a strong rank in hero Power. They differ in battles, refreshes and payouts.

| Arena | Free battles a day | Paid refreshes | Can buy battles? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lowest | 5 | Up to 10 | Yes |
| Middle | 10 | Up to 10 | Depends on the arena |
| Upper | 10 | Up to 5 | Depends on the arena |
| Top | 30 | None | No |

Two more rules matter. In the lowest arena, only a player in the **top 10** can challenge from the rank table, and the target must be ranked **50 or better**. Everyone else fights the opponents the arena offers.

> **Note:** **Triangular Arena** is confirmed by the official update notes of 30 July 2026. Servers in the Era of Revival also get **Supreme Duel**, a 1v1 tournament across 16 warzones from Era day 58. See [Era of Revival](#/era).

> **Unverified name:** Player videos name a **Rookie Arena** (a "5/5 challenges" run, which matches the lowest arena), a **Triangular Arena**, an **Arena of Fate** (shown paying Fame Medals and UR shards) and an **Apex Arena**. The battle and refresh numbers above come from datamined client files, which do not use those names. Which name belongs to which row is not confirmed; check the arena list in-game.

## Rewards

### Season payout by rank

Each season closes with a payout by rank, and it reaches very far down the ladder, so almost everyone active gets something.

| Arena | First place | Lowest paid rank |
|---|---|---|
| Lower two arenas | 1,010 diamonds | 85 diamonds, down to rank 15,000 |
| Middle | 1,000 diamonds and 5,000 Skill Badges | 50 diamonds and 1,000 badges |
| Upper | 4,000 diamonds and 21,000 Skill Badges | 200 diamonds and 3,000 badges |
| Top | 15,200 diamonds | 3,000 diamonds, down to rank 200 |

Last place on the top arena still pays more than first place on the lower and middle arenas. That is why a climb into the higher arenas is worth planning for.

### Honor Shop

Arena medals are spent in the Honor Shop. Prices are datamined.

| Item | Medals | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| UR Gear Blueprint | 10,000 | 50 (exactly enough to take one gear piece to 4 stars) |
| Raven Essence | 800 | None listed |
| Universal Curio fragment | 200 | None listed |
| Refined Iron | 200 | 400 |
| Recruit Ticket | 2,000 | 100 |
| Awakening fragment | 2,500 | 25 |
| Universal UR hero fragment | 3,000 | 10 |
| UR supplies | 4,000 | 100 |
| UR Curio chest, UR Epigraph chest | 50,000 | 1 each |

**Buy UR Gear Blueprints first.** In-game currency cannot buy them anywhere else except seasonal event shops, while most other items on this shelf are also sold elsewhere. After blueprints, Raven Essence is the best rate.

> **Unverified:** The medal name is not settled. Shop guides and one player video call it **Fame Medals**, and those guides say it also comes from Canyon Conquest, Elixir Scramble and Royal City Scramble. The datamine calls the arena currency "glory medal" and its shop the "Glory Shop". Check the currency icon in the Honor Shop.

## The KMA plan

> **KMA:** The recommended routine is five free fights every day, the three free refreshes to find beatable targets, and zero diamonds on extra battles. Spend every medal on UR Gear Blueprints until the 50 are bought.

Buying battles is the classic waste. The price climbs **100, 200, 300, 400, then 500 diamonds** each. Five bought battles cost 1,500 diamonds and return at most 250,000 Antitoxin in the lowest arena. Those diamonds do far more almost anywhere else.

## Quick answers

**Is it worth fighting if I always lose?** Yes. A loss in the lowest arena still pays 25,000 Antitoxin, half a win.

**Does Arena Expert research help here?** No. Arena Expert is an Alliance Duel research node that doubles Duel points. It has nothing to do with the Arena.

**When does the season end?** The season length is not in any source we trust. Check the timer on the arena screen.

**Can I lose troops?** No. Only heroes fight here.
