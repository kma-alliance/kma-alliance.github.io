| | |
|---|---|
| Building | Arena, unlocks around Sanctuary 7 to 8 |
| Daily | 5 free battles. Missed fights are gone |
| Pays | Antitoxin per fight scaling with arena tier (up to 250,000 a day at the lowest arena), plus Fame Medals |

No dedicated guide exists for ranking tiers, defence rules or season resets; those are the things to note in-game.

## Honor Shop (Fame Medals)

| Item | Medals | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| UR Gear Blueprint | 10,000 | 50 (exactly enough to take one piece to 4 stars) |
| Raven Essence | 800 | – |
| Universal Curio fragment | 200 | – |
| Universal UR hero fragment | 3,000 | 10 |
| Awakening fragment | 2,500 | 25 |
| UR supplies | 4,000 | 100 |
| Recruit Ticket | 2,000 | 100 |
| UR Curio chest, UR Epigraph chest | 50,000 | 1 each |

Buy UR Gear Blueprints first. Fame Medals also come from Canyon Conquest, Elixir Scramble and the Royal City Scramble.

Extra arena battles cost escalating diamonds and are not worth it.
