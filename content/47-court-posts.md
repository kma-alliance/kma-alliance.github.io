The alliance that wins [Royal City Scramble](#/royal-city) takes the throne, and its leader becomes King. The King hands out court posts from **Royal City → Manage → Official Appointment**. Each post carries a standing buff for as long as it is held.

> **Unverified:** guide sites call this a "Temple Battle", but that name traces back to one site that invents English names, and it never appears in player videos or on official pages. The Temple itself is a hold objective in [Kingdom War](#/kingdom-war). The same site's English and Russian pages also swap two of the post names below, so check the buff on the appointment screen before you ask for a post.

| Post | Buff |
|---|---|
| King | Hero HP, ATK, DEF +5% |
| King of Battle | Hero stats +10%, enemy PvP death rate +5% |
| Chief of War | March speed +5%, enemy PvP death rate +5% |
| Queen | Build and research +20%, training +10% |
| Minister of Construction | Build +50%, research +25% |
| Minister of Development | Research +50%, build +25% |
| Chief of Affairs | Build and research +60% |
| Minister of Strategy | Infirmary capacity and healing +20% |
| Minister of War | Training capacity and speed +20% |
| Minister of Inner Court | Grain, timber, herb output ×2 |

King decrees cost 3,000 to 10,000 points with 7 to 14 day cooldowns. Battle goal stages sit at 10k, 20k, 40k, 70k and 100k points.

> **KMA:** If a friendly King is on the throne, the Diplomat asks for posts. Chief of Affairs and Minister of Inner Court are worth more to a growing alliance than the combat posts.
