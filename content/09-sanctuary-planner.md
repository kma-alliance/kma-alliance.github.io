## Why the prerequisite column matters most

The resources are the easy part. What actually stalls accounts is starting a fourteen-day upgrade and discovering the Research Lab is a level short. From Sanctuary 10 upward the Lab is a hard requirement, and Training Grounds and Alliance Hall gate specific levels.

Rows highlighted in red are ones your account cannot currently start. Fix those buildings first.

> **Warning:** From Sanctuary 22 up, do not begin an upgrade until the prerequisites for the **next two** levels are already in place. Otherwise the queue finishes and immediately sits idle.

## Stars are the other wall

Stars come from Clinic room upgrades and clearing the Cursed Forest, and **they cannot be sped up with anything**. Sanctuary 30 needs 326 of them. That is why the first advice in [Your first week](#/first-week) is to start the Clinic on day one even though the 4X side looks more interesting.

## Speeding it up, in order

Builder's Hut free time, then Alliance Help, then alliance tech, then city buffs (Sky Fortress is +20% construction), then survivor skills, and only then speedups. Save construction speedups for Alliance Duel day 2, where every minute scores 50 points.
