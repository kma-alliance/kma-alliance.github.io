## Surprise Encounter

Daily calls for supplies: 1 free call a day, daily cap 200, quick button does 5 at once. Calls also produce boxes that need **Surprise Emblems** to open.

Single-call odds: SSR Resource Supply 33%, 14× 5m speedup 23%, 62 Skill Badges 23%, 12 Study Scrolls 10%, 3 SR Antitoxin supplies 6%, **1 UR Hero Omni Shard 4.6%**.

Box contents by emblem tier (quality 2 box, 500 / 200 / 100 / 40 / 20 emblems):

| Reward | 500 | 200 | 100 | 40 | 20 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Skill Badges | 80,000 | 32,000 | 16,000 | 6,400 | 3,200 |
| 5m speedups | 4,800 | 1,920 | 960 | 384 | 200 |
| UR Omni Shards | 120 | 48 | 24 | 10 | – |
| Raven Essence | 500 | 200 | 100 | 40 | 20 |
| UR Resource Supply | 200 | 80 | 40 | – | – |
| Study Scrolls | 2,400 | 960 | 480 | – | – |

Across all boxes that is 481,600 badges (more than all Hero Growth Tasks), about 101 days of speedups and 3,096 Raven Essence. The 500-emblem tier has the best shard ratio, so save emblems for it, but do not hoard them forever.

## Hero and Raven Wishing Wheels

1 Wish Coin per spin, 10 for 10, one free coin daily.

- **Hero wheel:** gearstones ×120 21.5%, Antitoxin supply ×11 21%, Skill Badges ×110 13%, gearstones ×180 11%, Cynthia shards about 31% combined (mostly ×1, 1% ×5, 0.1% ×100), UR Sword 0.2%.
- **Raven wheel:** Raven Essence ×2 26%, 1k Raven Fruit ×6 42%, Lv2 Raven Gear Chest 21%, 10k Fruit ×2 6%, Essence ×10 2%, 100 UR Omni Shards or a Lv5 Raven Gear Chest 0.1% each.

Take the free coin every day. Do not buy coins.
