Surprise Encounter looks like a small daily button. It is actually one of the best free sources in the game. Each call pays a small reward on the spot and can drop boxes, and those boxes, opened with **Surprise Emblems**, are where the value is. What decides success is simple: **take the free call every day** and open your boxes in the right way instead of letting emblems sit.

This page also covers the **Wishing Wheels**, two small prize wheels with a free coin each day. They are a side activity, not the main way to get heroes.

### Opening boxes: what the emblem count changes

This is the part most players get wrong. The payout **scales in step with the emblems you spend**, so each emblem is worth about the same at every level. A 500-emblem box is not a better rate than a 100-emblem box. What changes is **which items appear**: the smaller boxes leave some items out.

| Quality 2 box reward | 500 | 200 | 100 | 40 | 20 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Skill Badge | 80,000 | 32,000 | 16,000 | 6,400 | 3,200 |
| 5m Speedup | 4,800 | 1,920 | 960 | 384 | 200 |
| UR Hero Omni Shard | 120 | 48 | 24 | 10 | None |
| Raven Essence | 500 | 200 | 100 | 40 | 20 |
| SSR Antitoxin Level Supply | 400 | 160 | 80 | 32 | None |
| UR Resource Supply | 200 | 80 | 40 | None | None |
| Study Scroll | 2,400 | 960 | 480 | None | None |
| 10K Raven Fruit | 320 | 128 | 64 | None | None |
| UR Curio Chest | 10 | 4 | 2 | 1 | None |
| Lv.5 Raven Gear Choice Chest | 5 | 2 | None | None | None |

A quality 1 box pays roughly three quarters of these amounts: 60,000 badges and 90 Omni Shards at 500 emblems, for example.

So the rules are: **open at 100 emblems or more** so you get every item, save your big emblem stacks for **quality 2 boxes**, and do not hoard emblems waiting for a better rate that does not exist.

## The Wishing Wheels

There are two wheels. One spin costs 1 coin, ten spins cost 10, and **a free coin arrives every day**. Neither wheel has a documented pity counter.

> **Note:** The Wishing Wheel is **not** the main hero pull. Heroes come mainly from **summoning with Recruit Tickets**, which guarantees a UR shard reward (10 shards of a UR hero) every 50 summons. See [Building heroes](#/hero-building).

## Quick answers

**Should I save emblems for the 500 box?** No need. Rewards grow in step with emblems, so 100 and 500 give the same value per emblem. Only go below 100 if you have no choice.

**Which wheel should I spin?** Hero wheel if you are building Cynthia or need Gearstone and badges; Raven wheel if Raven upgrades are your bottleneck.

**Is the wheel how I get UR heroes?** No. Summoning with Recruit Tickets is the main path, with a guaranteed UR shard reward every 50 summons.

**Is it worth diamonds?** Nothing in trusted sources prices extra calls or coins. The free call and free coin are the sure value.
