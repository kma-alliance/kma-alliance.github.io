> **Unverified name:** "Kingdom Clash" appears only on guide blogs. They describe a quest-point race between matched kingdoms announced in March 2026, and the official patch notes of that same week added **Kingdom War** with exactly that description. So "Kingdom Clash" is almost certainly Kingdom War under a blog name. The **Revival Expedition League** is confirmed as part of the Era of Revival.

This page covers the two cross-server formats guides talk about. One of them turned out to be an event you already know. The other is the long league that runs through your Era season, where what decides your part is **keeping your best march alive and ready every day**.

## How the Revival Expedition League works

When your server enters the [Era of Revival](#/era), the Expedition League opens on Era day 1 and runs to the end of the season. Kingdoms 33 to 64 entered on 13 July 2026, and later servers follow in turn.

What is solid:

- **It belongs to the Era.** The datamined season page lists the Expedition League among the systems open from day 1 to the end, a creator's Era preview gives it its own chapter, and official notes changed its length.
- **Era rankings pay at the end.** Official notes describe Era settlement rewards, with the top 20 alliances receiving Castle of Revival items, and an Era Alliance Ranking screen.
- **The Era is cross-server.** A creator's preview of the Era lists the Royal City becoming cross-server, and official notes mention a **Sandbox** where alliances from different servers appear together.

What rests on guide blogs only: each player fights with their **single strongest march**, troops should be **healed before the daily reset**, stamina should be saved, and rewards include Era Currency, awakening and exclusive weapon materials, Curios, MR Gear Blueprints and Era skins.

> **Unverified:** guide blogs say the League **replaces** Kingdom War. But a creator's live streams titled "Era Of Revival KVK", on Era days 36 and 54 of his server, show kingdom-versus-kingdom wars still running inside the Era. Plan for both until your own event screens show which one you have.
