Both of these are documented only by pre-launch guides, so treat the details as provisional and tell an officer what you see in-game.

## Kingdom Clash

- Announced 24 March 2026 as the game's first cross-server battle.
- **Week-long.** Your whole kingdom versus one matched kingdom of similar server age.
- **Quest-based scoring.** Alliances complete designated quests for Kingdom Points that accumulate daily into one kingdom total. Highest total wins Honor rewards. Likely quest categories: building, research, training, gathering, combat.
- No published reward table.

Play it like a Duel week: stockpile speedups and resources beforehand, keep daily participation steady across time zones, and watch the enemy score.

## Revival Expedition League

For servers that have entered the **Era of Revival** (rolling out from 13 July 2026 to servers older than about 130 days).

- Replaces Kingdom War as a cross-kingdom battleground of **16 kingdoms**, seasonal and leaderboard-ranked, running the whole expansion.
- Each player deploys their **single strongest march**. Heal before the daily reset. Coordinate to avoid losses.
- Awakening materials are among the rewards.

See [Era Pass and Era of Revival](#/era) for the systems that come with it.
