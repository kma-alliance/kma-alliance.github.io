## Preparation phase

Donate **Mouse Lure** for a rally damage buff: 15 lures +3%, 20 +7%, 25 +12%, 30 +18%, 35 +25% (cap). Donations also pay Alliance Contribution.

## The rallies

- Each rally lasts 3 minutes. With travel time, a cycle is about 3 minutes plus twice the march.
- **Two lineups per player**: one marching while the other queues. That fits 45 to 50 rallies into the window.
- Officer-led rallies get +5% (R5 or title holder) or +2.5% (R4). Members join, they do not start their own.
- Offline members auto-join 20 seconds before launch if Offline Joining is on.

## Rewards

Personal thresholds pay speedups, diamonds (45 to 90), gearstones (200 to 740) and Refined Iron. Alliance thresholds pay Antitoxin and resources. The **top damage dealer on each trap** gets the alliance reward multiplied 2 to 10 times, so the best hitters should focus on one trap.

## KMA plan

> **KMA:** When an officer calls Cheese Trap, join with two lineups and stay for the full 30 minutes. Lure donations open the day before; donate early so the buff is at 25% when the trap starts.

## Mistakes

- Joining Trap I with your best squad when the alliance wanted it on Trap II.
- One lineup only, which halves your rally count.
- Leaving before the timer ends; the last minutes are where stacked lures matter most.
