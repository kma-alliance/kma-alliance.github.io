## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Opens | Server day 15, Sanctuary 10 |
| Needs | 15 registered members with level 10+ territories |
| Format | Six alliances per group, 5 round-robin rounds. Win 3 points, draw 1, loss 0, bye counts as a win |
| Lanes | Left, Mid, Right. 15 players per lane (20 in Elite and above) |
| Cost | No soldiers lost, no march queue used |

## How a lane fights

Players in a lane fight in Power order. Each one beats up to two opponents then withdraws. Winning a lane is worth 3 points; take two of three lanes to win the round. Tiebreak is total deployed Power. Arrangements lock when the entry phase starts; if capacity is limited the higher-Power troops deploy first.

Rank 1 in a group gains 750 medals, rank 6 loses 500. Fame Medals for rank 1 run from 8,000 to 16,000 by league. Members get rewards even without deploying, but leaving after the match forfeits them.

## KMA plan

> **KMA:** Officers assign lanes. Stack two lanes with the strongest players and concede the third. Lanes can be reassigned any time before battle, so tell an officer if you will be offline.
