## Priority in one paragraph

Rush the **Sanctuary** because it gates hero level caps, buildings and troop tiers. Keep the **Research Lab** one level below your target Sanctuary level (hard prerequisite from level 10 up). Keep **Training Grounds** at its prerequisite levels (7, 8, 15, 25, 30) because it decides your troop tier. Start the **Clinic and forest clearing** on day one for Stars. Build enough **Herb Gardens and Farms** that you never stall on herbs. Push the **Smelting Workshop** toward 25. Keep the **Infirmary** never behind the Barracks.

The generic "production first, storage second, Sanctuary third" advice you will see on content sites is a 4X template. Experienced players on this game put the Sanctuary, Lab and Training Grounds first and treat resource buildings as visible-score padding.

The [Sanctuary planner](#/sanctuary-planner) totals the bill from your level to any target and flags the prerequisites that will block you.

Every number here is generated from the site's data layer, so the table, the planner and the dashboard can never disagree. A `~` marks a value interpolated between published levels; a `*` marks an interpolated star threshold. **Build times are base times**, before any construction-speed buff, so yours will be shorter.

**Stars are a running total, not a per-level price.** They are never spent. You hold the threshold, upgrade, and keep them for the next level. There are 326 in the whole game, and Sanctuary 28, 29 and 30 all need all 326. Never add these rows together.

Reaching Sanctuary 17 costs about **44.5 million** grain plus the same again in timber, **14.8 million** herbs, and **159 hours** of base build time on one queue. Levels 6 and 7 are the early spikes.

> **Warning:** From Sanctuary 22 up, do not start an upgrade until the prerequisites for the next two levels are already prepared. A 14-day upgrade that lands on a blocked Lab is two weeks wasted.

|---|---|---|---|

| 2nd Workbench | Sanctuary 9 | 1 | Second parallel research queue. Never idle. |

| Barracks | Sanctuary 6 | up to 3 | Troop capacity. |
| Infirmary | Sanctuary 7 | up to 3 | Heals severely wounded. Overflow wounded die. Keep at or above Barracks. |
| Soldier's Rest | Sanctuary 1 | 1 | Converts fallen troops into resting soldiers who return. Cuts revive cost. |
| Walls | Sanctuary 4 | 1 | City defense and a prerequisite at 5, 6, 9 and 10. Stay within 5 levels of the Sanctuary. |
| Watchtower | Sanctuary 8 | 1 | March warnings and Scope Level, which hides your troops from enemy scouts. |
| Alliance Hall | Sanctuary 5 | 1 | Help count per queue: 4 at level 1, 20 at level 30. Prerequisite at 7, 8, 9 and 20. |
| Antitoxin Workshop | Sanctuary 5 | 1 to 5 | Hero XP. 1,556 an hour at level 1, 259,666 at level 30. Tap to collect. |
| Smelting Workshop | Sanctuary 5 | up to 5 | Gearstones for hero gear. Push to 25. |
| Herb Garden | Sanctuary 1 | up to 4 (2nd at Sanctuary 2, 4th at 19) | Herbs enter Sanctuary costs at level 9 and never leave. |
| Farm and Lumberyard | Sanctuary 1 | up to 4 each | Grain and timber. |
| Granary, Lumber Depot, Herb Storage | Sanctuary 3 | 1 each | Protected resources (raid-safe amount). |
| Falcon Tower | Sanctuary 6 | 1 | World map, chat, Falcon Quests. |
| Warrior, Warlock, Ranger Statues | Sanctuary 7, 11, 12 | 1 each | Faction hero stats and up to 100 leadership each. |
| Black Ops | Sanctuary 9 | 1 | Unlocks Covert Operations, the main hero XP source. |
| Raven Nest | Sanctuary 7 | 1 | The Raven is your sixth fighter. Raven Essence levels it, and Epigraphs slot into it. |
| Hall of Honor | Sanctuary 1 | 1 | Open from the start and capped at level 1. A shard sink for maxed heroes, so it sits unused for months. |

## Construction speed, in order of use

Builder's Hut free time → Alliance Help → alliance tech nodes → city buffs (Sky Fortress +20%) → survivor skills → speedups last. VIP 5 gives +15% build speed and VIP 8 gives +30%.

Save construction speedups for Alliance Duel day 2 (and days 5 to 6) where each minute scores.
