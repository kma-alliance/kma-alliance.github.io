## The short version

1. **Arthur** first. Every player, every budget. He is the most farmable UR (in every recruit pool's guarantee and the Alliance Shop) and simulations show a squad drops from 100 to 64 effectiveness without him.
2. **Marlena** second. Top burst damage dealer. She comes in the $1 first top-up pack and is the only UR that also accumulates shards for free.
3. Then one of **Shadow** (spenders), **Harper** (Warrior teams, Daily Offer on server day 22), or **Stellar** (the only SSR healer, key for Rat Swarm).
4. SSR depth: **Grenwald** for damage if you have no Marlena, **Bella** or **Griffith** as the second tank.

> **KMA:** Alliance policy is that every member builds Arthur first. Officers will ask what star level he is at when we plan Rat Swarm and Undead Siege stages.

A fully invested A-tier hero beats a half-built S-tier hero in every mode. Do not spread shards.

## Factions and the counter triangle

Heroes belong to one of three factions. The counter is a flat 20% damage swing.

| Attacker | Beats | Takes 20% less from |
|---|---|---|
| Warrior | Warlock | Warlock |
| Warlock | Ranger | Ranger |
| Ranger | Warrior | Warrior |

Faction bonus for the whole squad's HP, ATK and DEF: 3 of one faction +5%, 3 plus 2 of another +10%, 4 of one +15%, 5 of one +20%.

## SR starters

Durant, William, Robin, Kafa. Bench them the moment an SSR arrives. Never put Antitoxin into them.

## Who to build at each budget

| Budget | Order |
|---|---|
| Free | Arthur (farm shards from pools and the Alliance Shop) → Grenwald or Marlena if you will spend $1 → Bella from the Alliance Shop → Claire → Stellar. Aim for all-Warrior +20%. Best no-UR squad from simulations: Celia, Lucius, Sivir, Bella, Hastar. |
| Low spender | Monthly pass first for diamonds → Arthur and Marlena banners → Harper on server day 22 → VIP 5. |
| Heavy spender | Arthur → Marlena → Shadow → Cynthia. One banner to pity before starting the next. |

## Skip list

- Ash, Sivir, Kesso, and Lucius unless you need the all-Warrior bonus.
- Any SR once you own SSRs.
- Pulling every new UR banner. Depth beats breadth.

## Where the numbers come from

The rankings merge seven public tier lists. Sites written before July 2026 only know about 20 heroes. The wiki ranks purely by maximum Power, which favours tanks; opinion sites rank Marlena highest because her damage multiplies. Both are right in their own way. Arthur plus Marlena is the only universal consensus.
