| | Abyss Boss | Weekend Boss |
|---|---|---|
| Attempts | 10 per boss cycle | 3 |
| Faction rule | Boss favours Ranger, Warlock or Warrior: that faction's hero damage +50% | Same |
| Wrong faction | Damage halved | Same |
| Rewards | Speedup tasks (about 92 days of speedups over the task track), plus drops | Not published |

Unlock level, HP, reset time and full reward tables are not published. Note them in-game.

## Plan

Build at least one squad per faction over time. Never send an off-faction squad; half damage is not worth an attempt. The boss screen tells you which faction to field. Use every attempt.
