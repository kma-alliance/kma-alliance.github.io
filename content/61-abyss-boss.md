These are solo bosses that each **favour one faction**: Warrior, Ranger or Warlock. Heroes of that faction deal +50% damage, and a squad that does not match loses half its damage. So the whole event comes down to one choice made before you tap attack: **which faction's squad you send**. Everything else is using every attempt.

> **Warning:** This is the **opposite** of the [Demon King](#/demon-king). Against the Demon King you bring the faction that counters it. Against the Abyss and Weekend Boss you bring the **same** faction the boss favours. Mixing the two rules up is the most common way to waste a whole day of attempts.

## Rewards

Abyss Boss rewards come mainly from **boss tasks**, a track of goals that pays as you progress. These are one-time totals: once the track is done, it is done, which is why pushing it steadily matters.

| Reward | Total on the Abyss Boss task track | Largest single payout |
|---|---|---|
| Skill Badges | 317,000 | 50,000 |
| Gearstone | 230,000 | 50,000 |
| Speedups | About 92 days | Not published |
| SSR Epigraph fragments | 2,450 | 450 |
| Tempered Steel | 2,000 | 500 |
| Recruit Tickets | 705 | 150 |
| Raven Essence | 400 | 160 |
| Universal awakening fragments | 80 | 40 |

The Weekend Boss pays **388 Raven Essence** in total across its drops, 21 at most in one go.

## The KMA plan

> **KMA:** The recommended approach is to build one solid squad per faction over time, send only the matching squad to each boss, and use every attempt. If you do not yet have a full squad for the boss's faction, try your best heroes of that faction before falling back on your main off-faction squad.

You do not need three maxed squads. A matched squad of weaker heroes can still beat a strong squad that loses half its damage. Start with your main faction, then add a second, then a third.

## Quick answers

**Which faction do I bring?** The one the boss screen names. Same faction, not the counter.

**I only have a Warrior squad. Should I skip the Ranger boss?** No. Attempt it with your best Rangers. Unused attempts pay nothing, and a matched squad avoids the half-damage penalty.

**Why only three attempts on the Weekend Boss?** That is the design. It makes each attempt matter more, so check the faction twice.

**What are the boss tasks worth?** A lot: 317,000 Skill Badges, 230,000 Gearstone, 705 Recruit Tickets and about 92 days of speedups over the whole track.
