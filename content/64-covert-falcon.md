## Covert Operations (Sanctuary 9)

- 70 operations by quality and secrecy. Heroes go out on timers and return hero XP, gearstones, materials and Antitoxin. The main hero XP source in the game: roughly 500k a day at level 1, 15M at max.
- Top op (quality 5, level 5): needs 3 heroes at level 110+, 2 quality-5 heroes, 1 hero at 4 stars; 4 hours; pays 12,177,000 hero XP and a UR Hero Omni Shard.
- 2 dispatches per squad. Without the Monthly Pass you have one squad fewer, so two fewer dispatches. Ops do not occupy a march queue.
- Others can intercept a running op for a share, up to 3 times. Daily cap 5 assists and 5 intercepts; an assist pays 10% of the main reward.
- Auto-dispatch at VIP 5, super mode at VIP 12.
- A UR Covert Op is worth 75,000 Alliance Duel points on day 2 and 6, and 2,000 Top Healer points. Hold Black Ops Tickets for those days.

Always run the most expensive op you can, and assist or intercept the most expensive ops you can see, not the first ones.

## Falcon Quests (Falcon Tower, Sanctuary 6)

- The tower refreshes every 6 hours; quests have 8 hours to complete; quality 1 to 5 sets the reward size.
- Free quests: Efficient Gathering, Supply Recovery, Pirate Legacy, Rescue Refugee, Celia's Trial. 10 energy: Blight Extermination, Aid Alliance Members, Eliminate Rebels, Rescue the Wounded. 20 energy: Defeat Blight Legion, Eliminate Threats.
- Tower level 1 shows 5 quests with 25 storage; level 15 shows 12 with 40 storage and needs 5,000 completed quests. Daily output 24 to 52 quests.
- Quality-4 chance rises from 18% at Sanctuary 1 to 41% at Sanctuary 35.

They feed Mystic Treasure fragments, Undead Siege clues, Top Healer (1,000 points) and Alliance Duel days 1, 3 and 5 (10,000 points each on claim). A completed quest with a red dot never expires, so **finish now, claim on the scoring day**. Storage fills in 18 to 30 hours, so never bank more than a day's worth.
