These two systems take a few taps a day and pay for a large share of your hero growth. **Covert Operations** send heroes away on timers for hero XP and shards. **Falcon Quests** are a refreshing quest board whose real value is timing: a finished quest can wait, unclaimed, for the Alliance Duel day that scores it.

> **Note:** **Black Ops** is the building that unlocks Covert Operations. It is not another name for the mode. Players shorten Covert Operations to "Covert Ops". Player videos show the Black Ops screen with two tabs, **Covert Ops Force** and **Treasure Digger**, the second being where [Alliance Excavation](#/alliance-boss-excavation) digs live.

## Covert Operations: how it works

There are **70 operations**, each with a quality and a secrecy level. Higher levels ask for stronger heroes and pay more. The requirement applies to the **whole party**, not one hero.

### Assists and intercepts

Assisting a friend's op or intercepting someone else's pays **one tenth** of that op's main reward. You get **5 assists and 5 intercepts a day**. Because the cap is small, spend it on the **most expensive op you can see**, not the first one in the list.

VIP 5 unlocks auto-dispatch and VIP 12 unlocks a faster mode that a player guide calls **Super Deploy**. The same guide warns to set its filter first, or it can send the wrong ops.

| Where Covert Ops score | Points |
|---|---|
| Alliance Duel days 2 and 6, per UR op | 75,000 |
| Top Healer, per UR op | 2,000 |

> **Note:** the [Alliance Duel](#/alliance-duel) guide lists 75,000 for a UR op. One community table lists 157,500, so the value may change between servers. Check the Duel task list.

## Falcon Quests: how it works

The **Falcon Tower** unlocks at Sanctuary 6 and also opens the world map, chat and scouting. Its board refreshes every 6 hours. Each quest has a quality from 1 to 5 and **8 hours** to finish. An unfinished quest disappears after 8 hours. A **finished but unclaimed** quest stays on the board with a red dot and, by player observation, never expires.

| Quest | Energy | Drops |
|---|---|---|
| Efficient Gathering | Free | Diamonds, grain, herbs |
| Pirate Legacy | Free | Resource Supply, Lv 1 Raven Gear Chest, Raven Essence |
| Supply Recovery | Free | Gearstone, Resource Supply, herbs |
| Rescue Refugee | Free | Survivor shards |
| Celia's Trial | Free | Antitoxin, Skill Badge, hero XP |
| Blight Extermination, Eliminate Rebels | 10 | Raven Fruit, Resource Supply |
| Aid Alliance Members, Rescue the Wounded | 10 | Resource Supply, grain and more |
| Defeat Blight Legion | 20 | Raven Fruit, Resource Supply, grain |
| Eliminate Threats | 20 | Resource Supply, Survivor Recruit Ticket, grain |

**Your Sanctuary level shapes the draw.** At Sanctuary 1, quality 4 is 18% of quests and quality 5 is 9%. At Sanctuary 35 they are 41% and 16%.

The tower levels up as you claim rewards, and level 15 takes 5,000 claims. Since the 30 July 2026 update, **one-tap reward claiming unlocks at Falcon Tower level 8** (official update notes). Higher levels show more quests and store more.

## Quick answers

**Do Covert Ops block my marches?** No. They do not use a march queue.

**Why do I have fewer op slots than my friend?** Each squad runs 2 ops, and without an active Monthly Pass the game counts one squad fewer.

**Will a finished Falcon Quest expire?** Players report that a finished quest with a red dot does not. An unfinished one vanishes after 8 hours.

**Why are my Falcon Quests better than last month?** Quest quality follows your Sanctuary level. Higher Sanctuary, more quality 4 and 5 quests.
