## Ranks and defaults

<div data-widget="alliance-org"></div>

| Rank | Seats | Default rule |
|---|---|---|
| R5 | 1 | Leader. Banner, code, language, transfer, disband, the four titles. |
| R4 | 6 (Alliance Gift 10 adds 2) | Launch events, lead rallies with the bonus, accept and kick below them, promote R1 to R3, alliance mail (needs Alliance Gift 5), declare war. |
| R3 | – | Auto-promoted from R2 at 1,000,000 Power. Auto-kicked after 96 h offline. |
| R2 | – | Auto-promoted from R1 at 500,000 Power. Auto-kicked after 48 h. |
| R1 | – | New members. Auto-kicked after 24 h. |

Member cap: 50 at alliance level 1, +5 per level, 100 at level 10. R4 and R5 are never auto-kicked. Leaders can change the auto-promote and auto-kick thresholds.

Joining pays the member 300 diamonds and, below Sanctuary 8, a free teleport next to the leader. There is a cooldown after leaving before you can join another alliance.

## Titles

Four honorary posts, one holder each, given to R4s by the R5. Sources disagree on the fourth name (Inspector or Goddess). No title gives a stat buff on its own; the only documented perk is the **+5% rally damage** that R5 and title holders share.

| Title | Job in KMA |
|---|---|
| God of War | Rally targets, march timing, war-day assignments for Duel day 6, Elixir Scramble and Kingdom War. |
| Diplomat | NAPs, tracking hostile and neutral alliances, talking to other leaders. |
| Recruiter | Sourcing and screening members, monitoring activity, flagging inactives. |
| Inspector / Goddess | Event participation, garrison readiness, stockpile checks before Duel days. |

## Alliance Hall and Help

The Alliance Hall opens at Sanctuary 6 (after Falcon Tower 1) and is a prerequisite at Sanctuary 7, 8, 9 and 20. Helps per queue: 4 at Hall 1, 20 at Hall 30. Each help shortens a timer by 30 seconds at level 1, 59 seconds at level 30, and the alliance tech "Enhance Help" adds up to 300 seconds per help.

Help applies to **construction, research and healing**, not training or forging. It is free for both sides and applies to timers already running when you join. The first 100 helps you give each day pay Alliance Coins.

## Contribution and alliance tech

- **Diamond contribution:** 25 a day, cost rising from 2 to 50 diamonds. All 25 costs 650 diamonds. Do what you can afford.
- **Alliance tech** has two trees. Development: construction, research and gathering speed, production, Enhance Help, and functions like auto-joining rallies offline. War: troop load, training speed, rally speed, ATK, DEF, HP.

> **KMA:** Donation order is Enhance Help and help-count nodes first (everyone benefits daily), then whichever branch the R5 has pinned in alliance mail.

## Alliance level and gifts

Alliance XP comes from member activity and pack purchases. Chests from packs and Elite Blight rallies raise the **Alliance Gift level** (20 levels): level 5 unlocks alliance mail, 8 gives caravans +10% speed, 10 adds 2 R4 slots, 15 adds "Claim All", 20 lets members ride caravans together. Golden Eggs split diamond pools when a member hits Sanctuary 8, 12, 16 (200 diamonds), 20, 24, 27 (500) or 30 (1,000).

## Alliance Shop

Currency: Alliance Coins. Refreshes weekly, reported on Mondays.

| Priority | Buy |
|---|---|
| High | UR Hero Omni Shards, Raven Essence |
| Medium | Bella shards (if you use her), Direct Relocate, 8-hour construction speedups, shields before a war week |
| Low | Raven Gear Chests (one source calls them must-buy, another low; hold them for Duel day 3 either way) |
| Skip | Basic steel, brass, iron, resource bags, low-value tickets |

## Territory

Alliance territory is the area around member Sanctuaries. Being inside it gives production, march speed and defensive bonuses. Teleport into the core, not the edge. Holding alliance crystal tiles adds up to 50 leadership per hero.

## Etiquette that every 4X alliance expects

- Join on day 1, help every time, do the daily contribution.
- Join officer rallies, do not start your own on bosses.
- Live in the core, keep a garrison set, keep Walls, Watchtower and infirmaries at requirement.
- Shield when offline in war weeks. Never leave troops outside during war day.
- Save items for the matching Duel day. Do not tap the hammer early.
- Honor NAPs. Hit assigned targets only.
- Call excavations. Bring two lineups to rally events.
- Tell an officer before going away so you are not auto-kicked.
