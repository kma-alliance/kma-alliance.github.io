Nobody in KMA has to spend. This page exists so that anyone who does spend gets the most out of it, and so free players know where their diamonds go.

## The economics

- Flat rate of about **100 diamonds per $1**, no bulk discount. The first top-up at each price point doubles the diamonds.
- 1,195 of 1,446 paid bundles return the full diamond value of their price plus extras. **Plain diamond packs are almost always the worst buy.**

## Order for a low spender ($5 to $30 a month)

1. **2nd build queue, $1.99.** Permanent. The single best purchase in the game.
2. **First top-up pack with Marlena, about $1.** The best-value hero purchase in the game.
3. **2nd research queue.** Permanent.
4. **Premium Monthly Pass, $24.99 per 30 days:** extra squad, +15% gathering, +5% build speed, daily diamonds, 2 extra covert-op dispatches, VIP status. Highest diamond-per-dollar of any recurring purchase. If the budget is tighter, the Weekly Diamond Pass.
5. **Development Contract, $19.99:** pays 50,000 diamonds across 12 payouts as your Sanctuary goes from 6 to 30. That is 25× face value, with more than half arriving at levels 28 to 30. Only buy it if you will reach Sanctuary 30.
6. **One UR banner at a time** (Arthur for new accounts, Marlena for established ones). Reach pity before switching.
7. Event packs only during the Alliance Duel or Survival Battle phase they score in.
8. VIP packs to hold VIP 5. Higher only if Status will stay on.

More build queues: 3rd for $9.99, 4th for $19.99. Both permanent.

## Never buy

- Raw resource packs.
- Sanctuary and cosmetic skins (zero stat value before the Era of Revival).
- VIP points bought with diamonds, except during rebate windows.
- Diamond speedups outside events.
- Extra arena battles.
- Spreading money across many heroes.
- Third-party top-up shops or accounts. Ban risk. Your call, but KMA does not recommend it.

## VIP

Two layers: **VIP Level** (permanent, from cumulative points) and **VIP Status** (time-gated). If Status lapses the level stays but the bonuses go offline. Status costs 3,000 diamonds for 7 days or 10,000 for 30, or comes with passes and packs.

| VIP | Points | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | 1,050 | Up to +8% grain, timber and herb output |
| 5 | 11,000 | **Auto-dispatch for Covert Operations**, +13% all production, +15% build speed, +25% gear forging speed, +5% march |
| 8 | 55,000 | Expedition battles, +8% march, +30% build |
| 11 | – | Universal UR hero fragments in the Diamond Shop |
| 12 | 550,000 | First combat stats: +4% hero HP, ATK, DEF; +10% healing; +18% research |
| 20 | 50,000,000 | +13% hero stats, +50% build and training speed |

**VIP 5 is where everyone should stop** unless they are a serious spender. Combat percentages only start at VIP 12.

VIP Shop priorities: Raven Essence, Universal Curio Shards, UR Hero Omni Shards, Direct Relocate.

## Free-to-play diamond order

1. Keep VIP Status active.
2. VIP points to level 5.
3. VIP Shop: Raven Essence, Universal Curio Shards, UR Omni Shards.
4. Direct Relocate to get into the alliance core.
5. A 24-hour shield in an emergency.

Skip the rest of the Diamond Shop. Free diamonds come from the Quiz of Wisdom, Dawn Breakout, Hero Road, Thief Leader rallies, Golden Eggs and the Era Covenant free track.

## Era Covenant (the season pass)

Each era is 56 days. The pass has 50 levels and can be finished in about 26 days. Free track: 4 Recruit Tickets and 5 UR Omni Shards. Pass: 45 tickets and 17 shards plus diamonds. Large Pass: 70 tickets and 34 shards. Recruit tickets are the headline reward.
