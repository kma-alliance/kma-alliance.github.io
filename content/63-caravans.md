## Your caravans

| | |
|---|---|
| Needs | Private Stable, Sanctuary 10 |
| Slots | 4 personal caravans, a 12-run list refreshing free once a day |
| Quality | N, R, SR, SSR, UR. Payout at level 25 runs from 2.2M (quality 1) to 13.2M (quality 6). UR shows gold on the map |
| Robbed | Standard caravans up to 2 times, top quality once, alliance caravans 3 times |

A UR caravan dispatch is worth 100,000 Alliance Duel points on day 2 (and day 6) and 5,000 Top Healer points, so hold Caravan Tickets for those days.

## Alliance caravans

R4 or R5 launch two six-carriage caravans. Boarding is open 4 hours, up to 20 members each, then a 2-hour journey. You must have been in the alliance more than a day. Only the coachman's squads defend, so the strongest member drives.

## Raiding other servers

Caravan Trade → **Foreign Caravan** tab lists caravans from other servers, with an option to exclude your own kingdom.

- **4 raids a day.** Each plunder is also worth 120 Kingdom Points in KvK weeks.
- Steal caravan crates and **Black Ops Chests**. Priority: UR Omni Hero Shards, then Antitoxin supply chests, then resources and coins.
- Raid foreign servers, not your own kingdom, and share targets by private message rather than public chat.
