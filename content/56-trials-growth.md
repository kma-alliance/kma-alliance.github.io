These two systems reward progress you are making anyway. You do not play them. You **check them and claim**, and you let them nudge where your upgrades go. What decides how much you get is simple: whether you remember to claim, and whether your hero stars land on heroes the tracks actually ask for.

### Trials

Trials has **52 tasks** that complete themselves as you upgrade. The source breaks down 30 of them; the rest include Epigraph Power milestones.

| Task type | Count |
|---|---|
| Gear Promotion | 9 |
| Exclusive Weapon reaches 1 to 7 stars (3 tasks per star) | 21 |
| Other tasks, including Epigraph Power | 22 |

The tasks pay **UR Hero Omni Shards**, a universal currency that can go into any UR hero. Unlocking a hero costs 10 shards, so this is one of the best free shard sources in the game.

For scale: taking **one** skill on a UR or SSR hero to its cap costs 436,700 badges. One finished Growth Task track covers a small slice of that, but it comes on top of upgrades you were making anyway.

## The KMA plan

1. **Claim after every upgrade session.** A gear promotion, an Exclusive Weapon star or a hero star can finish a task.
2. **Finish tracks, do not spread.** Shards on the two heroes of one track close it. The same shards spread across several tracks may close none.
3. **Pick pairs you would build anyway.** Arthur and Marlena (track 1001) are the classic early Warrior core. Joker and Louis (track 1010) are both in several tracks.
4. **Remember the cap.** Tracks only ask for up to 8 stars, so stars beyond 8 do nothing here.

> **KMA:** Growth Tasks are a hint, not a build order. Stay with the heroes the [Hero tier list](#/hero-tier-list) and your squad need. When two heroes are equally useful to you, choose the one in more tracks.

## Quick answers

**Do levels or gear count for Growth Tasks?** No. Only hero stars count, from 1 to 8.

**Where are the Omni Shards in Trials?** 40 come from the tasks and 14 from the score chests, 54 in all.

**Why is Cynthia rated so well for tasks?** She is in 4 tracks, so her stars count four times over. Joker is also in 4.

**Where do I find Growth Tasks?** The datamine places them in the bonus events window behind the Value Event button, under a tab it calls Hero's Trial. The tab name is a translation, so look for a tab listing hero star tracks.
