## Trials

52 challenges that tick off through ordinary progress: 9 Gear Promotion tasks, 24 Exclusive Weapon star tasks (3 per star from 1 to 7) and the rest unspecified. They do not auto-claim.

| Score | Reward |
|---|---|
| 100 | 2 UR Hero Omni Shards, 1,250 badges |
| 200 | 2 shards, 1,250 badges |
| 300 | 3 shards, 1,500 badges |
| 400 | 3 shards, 1,500 badges |
| 500 | 4 shards, 2,000 badges |

Total: 42,250 badges and 14 UR Omni Shards. Check it after every gear promotion and weapon star-up.

## Hero Growth Tasks

15 tracks of 10 steps (150 tasks). Each track names specific heroes and counts **stars only**, not levels or gear. Each finished track pays 25,000 Skill Badges and 22,500 gearstones, plus forging materials: 375,000 badges across all tracks.

Heroes that appear in the most tracks give the best return on shards: Cynthia 4, Joker 4, Arthur 3, Louis 3. Concentrate shards on the pair from one track. Spreading pays nothing until a step completes.
