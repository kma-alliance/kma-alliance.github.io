> **Unverified name:** neither "Exploration Chain" nor "Explorer Dungeon" appears in a player video or on any official page. Both come from a site that translates its own names from Russian. The numbers are datamined and reliable; the labels are not. In-game this is the sequence of numbered plots around your Sanctuary that you clear to expand the city.

## The expansion track

One-time, 153 steps around the city, about two thirds of them fights ("Banish the Plague" ×95), gated by recommended Power.

Totals: 6.9M hero XP, 1.33M herbs, 999k timber, 979k grain, 850 Resource Supplies, 700 Gearstone, **145 Recruit Tickets** (first at step 4, 29 ticket steps), 85 Celia and 70 Claire shards, 70 Tempered Steel. Push it whenever your squad clears the next step.

## The second idle bank

Unlocks at Sanctuary 2. A second 8-hour idle bank next to the one inside the walls. Its rate grows through 597 levels, from 24,000 to 1,750,020 Antitoxin an hour and 24,000 to 200,040 grain an hour. One full collection at max is 14,000,160 Antitoxin. 500 one-off secret levels add 371.7M Antitoxin, 29.1M herbs and 16.8M each of grain and timber.

Collect it every login along with idle income.
