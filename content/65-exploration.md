## Exploration Chain

One-time, 153 steps around the city, about two thirds of them fights ("Banish the Plague" ×95), gated by recommended Might.

Totals: 6.9M hero XP, 1.33M herbs, 999k timber, 979k grain, 850 Resource Supplies, 700 gearstones, **145 Recruit Tickets** (first at step 4, 29 ticket steps), 85 Celia and 70 Claire shards, 70 Tempered Steel. Push it whenever your squad clears the next step.

## Explorer Dungeon

Unlocks at Sanctuary 2. A second 8-hour idle bank next to the Clinic's. Its rate grows through 597 levels, from 24,000 to 1,750,020 Antitoxin an hour and 24,000 to 200,040 grain an hour. One full collection at max is 14,000,160 Antitoxin. 500 one-off secret levels add 371.7M Antitoxin, 29.1M herbs and 16.8M each of grain and timber.

Collect it every login along with idle income.
