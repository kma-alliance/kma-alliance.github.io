## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Needs | Sanctuary 7. Arrives on server day 2 |
| Format | 6 rounds per day, 4 hours each, 12 to 20 players of similar Sanctuary level per bracket |
| Themes | Build, Train, Research, Raven, Heroes, rotating through a 7-day table |

## Theme rotation

| Day | R1 | R2 | R3 | R4 | R5 | R6 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Build | Train | Research | Raven | Heroes | Build |
| 2 | Heroes | Build | Train | Research | Raven | Heroes |
| 3 | Build | Train | Research | Raven | Heroes | Build |
| 4 | Train | Research | Raven | Heroes | Build | Train |
| 5 | Research | Raven | Heroes | Build | Train | Research |
| 6 | Raven | Heroes | Build | Train | Research | Raven |
| 7 | Heroes | Build | Train | Research | Raven | Heroes |

## Scoring

| Theme | Points |
|---|---|
| Build | 1-minute speedup +10, +1 Building Might +1 |
| Train | 1-minute speedup +10, train one T9 +28 |
| Research | 1-minute speedup +10, +1 Tech Might +1 |
| Raven | 1 stamina +100, 10 Raven Fruit +1 |
| Heroes | one recruit +400, 1,950 Antitoxin +1 |
| Any | diamond pack +30 |

Survival Medals unlock daily chests at 2, 8 and 18 medals. Rank 1 in a round gets a UR Hero Omni Shard, an SSR Curio Chest and 24 SR grain supplies; ranks 6 to 20 get 3 supplies of each type. Rank 1 is also worth 2,200 Kingdom Points.

## The trick

Check which round theme is live when you are about to spend Duel items. When the Survival round matches the Duel day (a Build round on Tuesday, a Heroes round on Thursday), the same speedups or shards score in both. Officers post the alignment in the Sunday mail.
