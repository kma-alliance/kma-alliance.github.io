## Reading the result

**Shard numbers are exact.** They come from the published per-star table, which moves in 0.2-star steps: 2 shards a step in the first star, 60 a step in the tenth. That is why 8 stars costs 475 shards and 10 stars costs 975, with stars 8 to 10 alone costing more than stars 1 to 7 combined.

**Antitoxin numbers are close estimates.** Only certain levels are published (10, 30, 45, 60, 90, 148, 150), so the levels between them are interpolated on a curve that passes through every published anchor. The totals are the right order of magnitude and good enough to plan with.

## The two milestones that matter

- **Level 30 and 8 stars** together unlock the fifth skill. On a UR that is Tenacity, and it works from the bench, so a spare hero parked outside your five still gives the squad +20% ATK, HP and DEF.
- **10 stars** is the entry requirement for Hero Awakening once your server reaches the [Era of Revival](#/era).

## Where the income comes from

| Resource | Best sources |
|---|---|
| Shards | Alliance Shop (UR Omni shards weekly), recruit pools, Trials chests, Covert Operations, the daily Wishing Wheel coin, Lion Coin shop on Thief Hunt days |
| Antitoxin | Covert Operations first by a wide margin, then the Antitoxin Workshops, five free Arena fights, Undead Siege, and Level Supplies opened at a high Sanctuary level |
| Skill Badges | Hero Growth Tasks at 25,000 per finished track, Surprise Encounter boxes, Trials, Hero Road |
