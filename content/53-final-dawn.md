Final Dawn is a starter reward track. There is no fight and no ranking. It watches your **total Power** and pays a reward each time you cross one of eleven lines. What decides success is whether your Power reaches the top line **before the track closes**, and that depends on growing every part of your account at once.

## How it works

Power comes from everything a new player already does: training soldiers, building, research, hero upgrades, gear and the Raven. Final Dawn does not care which one it comes from. It only checks the total.

## The KMA plan

The recommended approach for a member on a new server:

1. **Do not specialise.** Push troops, buildings, research and hero levels together. The top lines need Power from every direction.
2. **Buy cheap Power first.** Research is uneven: the Development and Economy trees cost far fewer resources per Power point than Soldier or Hero research. See [Research](#/research).
3. **Use the rewards to grow.** The speedups go straight back into buildings, which raises Power again.
4. **Check the timer weekly.** If you are one line short near the end, spend held speedups and supplies to cross it.

> **KMA:** Recruit Tickets from this track are tempting to spend on the spot. If an [Alliance Duel](#/alliance-duel) is already running on your server, hero recruits score on Duel day 4, so claim them from Final Dawn when you reach them and hold the pulls for Thursday.

## Quick answers

**Do I need to do anything special?** No. It counts the Power you gain from normal play. Just claim the rewards.

**Does training troops count?** Yes. Soldiers, buildings, research, heroes, gear and the Raven all add Power.

**Can I still get it on an old server?** It is tied to the start of a server. If you do not see it in the Special Event tab, it has already closed on your server.

**Is Power the same as Might?** Yes. Some guide sites say Might; players and this handbook say Power.
