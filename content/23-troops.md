## How troops work here

Troops are a single pool with tiers T1 to T10. There is no infantry, cavalry, archer split. Factions (Warrior, Ranger, Warlock) belong to **heroes**, not troops. A hero's leadership stat decides how many soldiers march with it, and hero level is the main lever on that.

A smaller army of T10 beats a larger army of T8.

## The promotion trick

<div data-widget="promotion-trick"></div>

Max **one** Training Ground to your Sanctuary cap. Keep the other three at level 10, pumping T4. Each level-10 ground produces about 455 T4 in 10.5 hours. Then **promote** those T4 to your top tier on the maxed ground.

Example: 712 T4 promoted to T9 takes about 16.5 hours, versus 33 hours to train them from scratch. Roughly six to seven hours saved per cycle.

The wiki says the game has up to 3 Training Grounds; every guide describes four. The fourth is almost certainly a paid queue slot. Use as many as you have.

## Alliance Duel day 5

Training scores 20 points per T1 trained up to 110 per T10. Promotion steps count as training the new tier, so **promote one tier at a time** on day 5 and keep a stockpile of low-tier soldiers all week for it. Training speedups score 50 per minute on days 5 and 6.

## Healing

- **Infirmary** (Sanctuary 6 to 7, up to 3): heals severely wounded. Capacity is a hard cap; overflow wounded **die**. Three infirmaries hold about 6,090; four hold 8,120; research and lord skills add 45%.
- **Soldier's Rest** (Sanctuary 1): fallen troops become resting soldiers who return to the Barracks. Cuts revive costs.
- Healing costs about 30% of the recruit cost. A wounded soldier is three times cheaper than a dead one.
- Alliance Help works on healing queues. Healing speedups score 50 a minute on Duel days 5 and 6.
- The Royal City Scramble sends 70% of casualties to the infirmary instead of the grave.

Fill infirmaries to max capacity before a war week.
