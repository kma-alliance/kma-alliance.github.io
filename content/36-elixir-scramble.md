## Basics

- 30-minute 20-versus-20 alliance PvP on a separate symmetric map. Most points wins.
- Eligibility: top 32 alliances by Might on the server, alliance at least one week old, **Sanctuary 15** for the player.
- Roster: **20 starters and 10 reserves**, picked by R4 and R5. You cannot self-register. Matchmaking uses the **highest historical Might** of the roster, so one inactive whale drags the whole team into a harder bracket.
- Windows: 09:00, 18:00 and 23:00 server time, 30 minutes each. You spawn on the map 5 minutes early.
- Your own troops are not used. Event barracks give you troops of your highest unlocked tier. They cannot die; wounded go to a field tent and Healing Tents or Medkits recover them. Shielding inside the event is allowed.
- A win is worth **50,000 Kingdom Points** in Kingdom War.

A freshly captured structure is immune for 60 seconds, then contestable. Each holds up to 20 defenders.

## What each building is worth

Per second while held (alliance points / personal points):

| Structure | Points | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Elixir Castle | 80 / 30 | The core objective. About 48,000 points over the final 10 minutes. |
| Alchemy Workshop (x2) | 50 / 30 | Early lead |
| Healing Tent (x4) | 30 / 30 | Heals 15 wounded every 10 seconds |
| Observation Platform | 10 / 30 | **+10% to all alliance points** |
| War Relics | 10 / 30 | Allies +15% hero HP, ATK, DEF |
| Cursed Altar | 10 / 30 | Enemies -15% hero HP, ATK, DEF |
| Migration Portal | 10 / 30 | Relocation cooldown halved |
| Herb camps | 5 / 5 | Minor. For low-Might gatherers. |

Relics plus Altar together are a 30% stat swing in the Castle fight.

## The plan

> **KMA:** Roles are posted 30 minutes before the window. One shot-caller. No committee decisions mid-fight.

1. **0 to 10 min:** take the Observation Platform first (the multiplier only pays if you hold it early) and your own Workshop. Then contest the enemy Workshop with the spare squads.
2. **10 to 13 min:** everyone converges on the **Castle**. A second group takes Relics and Altar.
3. **13 to 30 min:** hold the Castle, rotate wounded through Healing Tents, low-Might players gather at herb camps.
4. Reserves stay ready to swap in for anyone who disconnects.

Everyone on the roster gets participation rewards. The winner gets the extra alliance rewards.
