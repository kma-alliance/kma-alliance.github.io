Dawn Breakout is the one fight in the game where a huge army does not help you. Every player hits the same **1,000-soldier cap**, so the result comes down to hero levels, skills, stars and gear. A small account with well-built heroes goes further than a big account that spent its season stockpiling troops.

## How it works

The event is a ladder: **3 runs, each of 5 levels**. Each level gives you **10 minutes**. Because the cap is shared, the numbers that decide a win are the ones on your hero screens, not your barracks.

The tickets are the prize. **Survivor Recruit Tickets** hire the survivors who staff posts inside your buildings. Hold the Level Supplies until your Sanctuary is higher, because they pay by level when opened.

## The KMA plan

The recommended approach is short, because the event rewards preparation more than play.

1. **Build heroes, not troops, before it opens.** Levels, skills, stars and gear on your main five all count. Troop count does not.
2. **Bring your highest troop tier.** The cap limits how many soldiers you bring, not which tier they are.
3. **Use all 3 runs and all 5 likes.** The likes cost nothing and take seconds.
4. **Save the tickets for the Duel.** Survivor recruits score on [Alliance Duel](#/alliance-duel) day 2 and 6, and in [Top Healer](#/top-healer). Hold them for those days if you can.

## Quick answers

**Does a bigger army help?** No. The cap is 1,000 soldiers for everyone. Heroes and troop tier decide it.

**What should I push if I am stuck?** The things the cap cannot touch: hero levels, skills, stars and gear on your main five. See [Hero building](#/hero-building).

**How many diamonds does it pay?** 80 per run, so 240 for all three.

**Why does this page carry a warning?** The name and numbers come from one datamined source only. If your client shows the same rewards under another name, tell an officer.
