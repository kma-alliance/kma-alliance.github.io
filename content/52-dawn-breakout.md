## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Cap | No more than 1,000 soldiers per fight, 10 minutes per level |
| Structure | 3 runs of 5 levels |
| Social | Like other players' results for a small currency, up to 5 likes a day |

## Rewards per run

| Reward | Qty |
|---|---|
| Survivor Recruit Ticket | 12 |
| Antitoxin Level Supply (SR) | 18 |
| Herb Level Supply (SR) | 18 |
| Antitoxin Level Supply (SSR) | 3 |
| Herb Level Supply (SSR) | 3 |
| Diamonds | 50 + 20 + 10 |

Three runs is 36 Survivor Recruit Tickets, which hire the survivors who staff building posts.

## How to play it

Because every player fights with the same 1,000-soldier cap, troop count is neutralised. Bring your best five heroes with the highest skills and gear you have, plus your best troop tier (1,000 T8 still beats 1,000 T5). Use all three runs and all five daily likes.

Hold the Level Supplies until your Sanctuary is high; they pay by level when opened.
