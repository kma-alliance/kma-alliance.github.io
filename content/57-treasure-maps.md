| Needs | Sanctuary 9 |
| Fragments | 7 distinct fragments, mostly from Falcon Quests |
| Odds | Gold 10%, Purple 30%, Blue 60% |
| Pity | A gold is guaranteed within 15 empty runs. The counter is hidden |

Keep Falcon Quests flowing; they are the fragment source. Do not quit after a dozen dry runs when the pity is at fifteen. Cooldowns and exact reward contents are not published, so note what you get and tell an officer.
