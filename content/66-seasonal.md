## The shape they take

Every seasonal event so far has had the same five parts in the Special Events tab:

1. A login and limited-pack track.
2. A currency you farm from normal play (Falcon Tower, monsters, blights, covert ops).
3. A gacha mini-game with one free key a day.
4. An exchange market with a territory or Sanctuary skin as the flagship.
5. Sometimes a server-wide meter with top-N rankings.

## Examples

**Easter 2026 (3 to 9 April):** Easter Celebration quests, an Egg Hunt with an exchange, an Art Market with efficiency buffs, login and packs. Rewards included a UR Hero Omni Shard, Raven Essence, Tempered Steel, the Easter Egg Warrior decoration and the Greenery territory skin. Code LAEGG26. Missed eggs and quests were not recoverable.

**Fantasy Realm (7 days):** Toy Keys (1 free a day) open the Wondrous Toy Box with milestones at 30, 120, 300, 600 and 1,000 opens. Cupcakes (up to 300 a day) feed a server-wide Sweetheart Banquet where the top 3 get a permanent +3,300 hero HP title. The Teacup Market sold the Rabbit Tea Party Sanctuary skin (4,000 teacups, +5% ATK and DEF, +5% hero ATK), a permanent title (2,000, +1.5% skill damage) and UR hero chests.

**Qixi (August 2026):** only the code LAQIXI26 is documented. No anniversary event has been seen yet; the game launched in early 2026, so watch for one.

## Plan

Do the free key and the daily currency cap every day of the window. Expand warehouses and wards first if the event rewards production. Spend all event currency before it ends; carry-over is not confirmed. Before the Era of Revival, skins have no stats, so buy them for looks only.
