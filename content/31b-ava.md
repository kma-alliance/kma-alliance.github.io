"AvA" is what players call the **Alliance Duel**: your alliance against another alliance for a week, one theme per day. It is not a separate event and it is not a stage of Kingdom War. What changes is **who** you fight and **whether there is a raid**, and that depends on how old your server is.

> **Note:** "AvA" is player shorthand. The game itself says **Alliance Duel**, and the official update notes call the cross-server version **Cross-Server Alliance Duel**. Every scoring detail, day by day, is on the [Alliance Duel](#/alliance-duel) page. This page is about how AvA grows up with your server.

For a server that opened on a Sunday, the first Monday on or after day 8 is **day 9**. For other opening weekdays it lands between day 8 and day 14. The [Server timeline](#/server-timeline) works out the exact date for your server once you enter your server day.

The game's own quiz data also uses longer theme names, such as **Enhance Raven**, **Territory Development**, **Tech Research** and **Enhance Heroes**, so you may see either in-game.

## What AvA is worth in Kingdom War

Once your server is in Kingdom War, the Duel is no longer just an alliance event. Every alliance Duel win adds to your kingdom's total.

## Quick answers

**Is AvA the same as KvK?** No. AvA is the Alliance Duel, alliance against alliance. KvK is [Kingdom War](#/kingdom-war), server against server. Once KvK runs, AvA wins feed it points.

**When does our first AvA start?** The first Monday on or after server day 8. Check the [Server timeline](#/server-timeline) for your date.

**Why is there no raid in our Duel?** Early Duels are on your own server and have no raid day. The raid arrives when Duels go cross-server around day 29.

**Can we pick our opponent?** No. The game matches alliances. On a new server the top alliances tend to meet each other first.
