## What it is

Waves of infected rats run **fixed paths** to your Sanctuary. You place and upgrade towers along the paths and field up to 5 heroes. The final wave of each difficulty tier is the **Rat King**. Beating it unlocks the next tier, better rewards and event scoring eligibility.

This is a solo mode, not an alliance event. The alliance "rat" events are Cheese Trap and Crystal Valley's Plague Rats.

## Choke points beat coverage

Find the 2 or 3 places where paths converge and stack upgraded towers there. A level 5 tower at a choke point beats five level 2 towers spread out. Every source agrees on this.

## Tower order at a choke

Guides disagree on the exact order, so here is the reconciled version:

1. A **slow or control tower** at the entrance of the choke.
2. An **AoE damage tower** immediately behind it, so slowed rats die in the kill zone. A slow tower with nothing behind it just delays rats.
3. Build the second choke the same way.
4. **Single-target** towers last, for the Rat King phase.

## Between attempts

- Repair damaged towers.
- Upgrade the key towers rather than adding new weak ones.
- Restock medicine before starting the wave.

## Hero lineup

| Slot | Hero | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Front | Arthur (or Bella) | Physical damage reduction for the team |
| Front | Griffith or Bella | Monster damage reduction aura |
| Back | Marlena | Main damage |
| Back | Stellar | Heals, buffs damage to monsters, death-trigger heal |
| Back | Claire, Bestar, Grenwald or Zoya | Flex damage. Zoya is the ideal wave clearer if you own her. |

Three maxed core heroes beat five mid ones.

## The Rat King

The fight is mostly about reaching the boss wave with more than half your team's HP. Hold every active skill through the trash waves. When the King spawns, fire Arthur's and Marlena's ultimates in the **first 10 to 15 seconds**. Survivability beats damage: teams that max damage die on the boss wave.

Readiness test: if waves 3 to 5 are comfortable, you are ready for the tier's boss.

Investment order for this mode: Arthur stars → Marlena stars → Stellar → second choke towers → hero skills → roster depth.
