## How it pays

Weekly Study Scroll payouts depend on the alliance's division **and** your rank inside the alliance.

| Division | Scrolls per week |
|---|---|
| Elite | 750 to 1,100 |
| Epic | 1,100 to 2,100 |
| Mythic | 2,100 to 3,120 |

Moving up a division nearly doubles the reward. Climbing from rank 100 to rank 10 inside the alliance adds much less. Promotion is the goal, which is another reason the whole alliance doing its Duel days matters.

Study Scrolls are the currency for the Alliance Duel research tree and the T10 troop node. See [Research](#/research).
