The League is not a separate event you play. It is a ladder built on [Alliance Duel](#/alliance-duel), announced on the official Facebook page on 11 June 2026. You play the same six Duel days. What changes is who you are grouped with and what a good week is worth, because weekly results **move the alliance between divisions**, and the division decides the payout.

The official announcement lists **Study Scroll, Raven Gear Chest and Gearstone** for everyone taking part, and a **Sanctuary Skin and Nameplate** for members of the top division. ## The KMA plan

> **KMA:** The League rewards the alliance, not the star. The recommended plan is simple: **everyone plays every Duel day**, saves items for the right day, and stays in the alliance until rewards arrive.

- **Promotion first.** Going from rank 100 to rank 10 inside the alliance adds 100 to 450 scrolls. Going up a division adds 350 to 1,100, usually about 1,000. Help weaker members finish their Duel milestones instead of chasing your own rank.
- **Top 30 matters a little.** Personal rank bands break at 10, 30 and 100. If you sit just outside a band, it can be worth the extra push.
- **Stay top 16.** Only the top 16 alliances in the kingdom enter each season. Keeping KMA's Power ranking up keeps us in.

## Quick answers

**Is the League a seventh Duel day?** No. The six Duel days stay the same. The League changes the grouping and the rewards.

**Our kingdom is 5 weeks old. Why no League?** Kingdoms need more than 7 weeks before the League opens.

**Does my own rank matter?** A little. Your rank inside the alliance picks the row in the reward table, but the alliance's division changes the payout far more.
