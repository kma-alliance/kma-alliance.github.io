> **Note:** the official update notes of 2 July 2026 call this event **Bandit Hunt**; the official update notes of 30 July 2026 call it **Thief Hunt**. Same event. Those notes also say **Thief Leaders at level 80 and above drop a Chief's Coin Chest**, given to the member who discovered the Leader and to the rally initiator, and that the highest Leader level you can discover rises after your alliance defeats the current highest Leader **3 times**. Only Leaders your alliance discovered, defeated by your members, count toward that.

> **Unverified name:** **Thief Leader** and **Lion Coins** are confirmed by player videos. "Gold Thieves" for the small solo targets comes from guide sites and one player's video description, but no source shows it as an on-screen label, and another player guide calls them "thief clusters". This page says "small thieves".

Thief Hunt puts thieves on the world map that are normally hidden. During the event everyone can see them, and a button in the event window takes you to the nearest one. You catch the small thieves solo for **Lion Coins** and Raven Fruit, and while they are being caught a much stronger **Thief Leader** can appear, which the alliance takes down with rallies. Coins go into an event shop. Players report the event lasts **3 days** and returns about every **3 weeks**, and unspent coins carry over. What decides how much you get is **energy discipline**, **joining every Leader rally**, and **buying the right things** in the shop.

Leader levels run from **5 to 135**. After you beat a Leader, the next one you find is higher, about 5 levels by one player's report. Each Leader kill also gives an energy item that refunds what you spent.

### Thief Leader ranking

Damage to the Thief Leader adds up into a server ranking, and players report that **damage counts even when the rally fails**. The higher your place, the bigger the reward, delivered by mail after the event. Everyone who landed **at least one hit** gets a participation reward.

> **Unverified:** the ranking prizes are not settled. An older guide listed up to 10 × 2,500 Alliance Coins plus SR supplies at the top; one player names medals as the best ranking reward. Check the ranking tab.

## The KMA plan

1. **Split your squads.** A weaker squad catches small thieves. Your strongest squad leads rallies on Leaders you find, before other alliances spot them. If you have a third, keep it home to join rallies.
2. **Join every Leader rally.** Joining costs no energy and pays coins, and your damage counts for the ranking. Joining as the third or fourth player spares troops, because the first squads in take the losses.
3. **Post Leaders you cannot kill.** Share them in alliance chat inside the 5-minute window, so KMA gets the kill instead of a rival alliance. Never take a Leader another KMA member found.
4. **One member per thief.** Two alliance mates chasing the same small thief waste a march.
5. **Arrive with a full energy bar and full barracks.** Energy refills 1 every 6 minutes and stops at 120, so a full bar takes about 12 hours. Ease off spending half a day before, but do not let it sit full and waste the refill.
6. **Hold the Raven Fruit.** Feed it to the Raven on Alliance Duel day 1 (Raven), where it also scores points.

> **KMA:** The recommended plan is solo thieves on one squad and Leader rallies on another, with officers posting each Thief Leader in alliance chat. Spend Lion Coins on Omni Shards and Recruit Tickets before anything else.

## Quick answers

**Does joining a Thief Leader rally cost energy?** No. Joining is free. Starting a rally costs 20 energy, and a failed rally still spends it.

**What if I run out of energy?** Keep joining Leader rallies, and buy the three 50 Stamina packs in the shop for 10 coins each.

**What is the best thing to buy?** Hero Awaken Omni Shards (120) and Recruit Tickets (80), both limited to 10.

**Do unspent Lion Coins disappear?** Players report they carry over to the next Thief Hunt.
