> **Unverified:** sources disagree on how Kingdom War runs. This page follows guides describing a points week and a 4-hour Saturday war. Datamined data instead describes a hold-time war: every second holding the Temple or Throne scores a point, 7,200 points wins, and four corner towers each speed the count by 25%. Kingdom War durations conflict between sources, including inside the datamine itself. Check your kingdom's event screen before planning around a time.

## Points Battle

| Activity | Kingdom Points |
|---|---|
| Alliance Duel victory | 30,000 |
| Alliance Duel MVP | 6,000 |
| Demon King: kingdom total damage win | 250,000 |
| Demon King: personal damage #1 | 150,000 |
| Elixir Scramble win | 50,000 |
| Survival Battle #1 | 2,200 |
| Caravan plunder | 120 each, 4 attempts a day |

Aim for 7 to 8 reward tiers a day. Hoard Falcon rewards, Raven Essence, speedups, recruit tickets and gear chests for the matching Duel day, because the Duel is the biggest point source.

## War day

The points winner attacks the loser's Royal City with a **4-hour window** to reach 100% occupation. Capture the turrets on the way in; enemy-held turrets shred the city's defenders. **No shields during war.** Troops left outside cities die fast. Allies garrison the Royal City.

Rank 1 kingdom: 3 UR Curio Chests, 18 Universal Curio Shards, 55,000 Fame Medals and a 13-day frame. Win streaks double rewards and reset on a loss.

## KMA plan

> **KMA:** War week rules. Save speedups all week. Be online at war start. Follow calls in the kingdom channel. Hit shared targets only. Keep troops inside cities or at home. Rally captains are published before Saturday.

Officers coordinate with the other alliances in the kingdom: NAPs, territory assignments, event scheduling across time zones.

## Mistakes

- Free-fighting instead of reinforcing the assigned structure.
- Leaving a march outside during war day.
- Burning Duel items early in the week for personal score.
