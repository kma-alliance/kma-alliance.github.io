## The three layers

1. **Army power:** troop tier (T7 to T8 as a mid-game bar), hero level (100 as a baseline), Lab research for HP, ATK and DEF.
2. **Alliance protection:** live inside the KMA core, be covered by NAPs, be reachable for reinforcement.
3. **Shields.**

## Garrison

World map → tap your Sanctuary → **Reinforcement → Garrison** → tick the squads to defend. Or Walls → Fortifications. Higher garrison Might means more Undead Siege waves survived and better odds against raids.

Garrison squads should be tank-heavy with sustain. Long fights favour damage reduction over burst. See [Squads and team comps](#/team-comps).

## Reinforcing

Send squads to an ally's Sanctuary or Alliance Hall. They defend until recalled. Reinforcements onto a city or fortress stack if your alliance already holds it. Reinforcing triggers War Frenzy on you.

## Walls and Watchtower

- **Walls** are a Sanctuary prerequisite from level 4. Keep them within 5 levels of the Sanctuary.
- **Watchtower** (Sanctuary 8) warns you of incoming marches and raises your **Scope Level**, which hides your troop composition from enemy scouts. Enemy reports show your Hall of Honor Might from Watchtower 13 and details from 14. Upgrade it before living in a contested zone.

## Scouting

Your report detail depends on your Watchtower level and is hidden by the target's Scope Level. Scouting **triggers War Frenzy** on you. Before Elixir Scramble or war day, officers scout the enemy roster: member count, positioning, strongest player.

## Shields

Full price table is in [Resources](#/resources). The short version:

- Seven free 8-hour shields from Sanctuary levels, plus 5 free 8-hour charges a week.
- Alliance Coin shields: 8 h for 7,500, 12 h for 9,900, 24 h for 19,800. The 24-hour is the best per hour.
- Diamond shields are the emergency option: 1,500 for 8 hours.

**When to shield:**

- Every time you log off during a war week.
- Alliance Duel day 6 if you are not a fighter.
- Whenever your troops are home and you are a resource target.

**War Frenzy:** any military action (scout, reinforce, deploy, attack, siege) blocks shielding for **15 minutes**. Plan your last action at least 15 minutes before bed.

**Shields do nothing** in the Royal City Scramble, on Kingdom War war day, and against Undead Siege blights.

## Infirmary

Overflow wounded die permanently. Three infirmaries hold about 6,090, four hold 8,120, research and lord skills add 45%. Fill them to max before a PvP week. Healing costs about 30% of recruit cost, so a wounded soldier is three times cheaper than a dead one. Alliance Help works on healing.

## Cities

86 capturable cities in six levels. Bonuses are alliance-wide and mostly economic:

| Level | City | Bonus |
|---|---|---|
| 6 | Lionheart Fortress | Training speed +5% |
| 6 | Nightfall Fortress | Research speed +20% |
| 6 | Sky Fortress | Construction speed +20% |
| 5 | Temple of Chaos | Timber output +20% |
| 5 | Temple of Nature | Grain gathering +20% |
| 5 | Temple of Order | Herb output +20% |
| 5 | Temple of War | Grain output +20% |

Holding a level 6 city is the ticket to the Royal City Scramble. Level 6 cities have 200,000 HP and a garrison of 100; level 5 have 150,000 HP and 80. After a fortress falls there is an occupation phase where squads get knocked out and slots reopen, so reinforcement rotation matters.

## Servers and relocation

There is **no server transfer** as of September 2026. Characters are bound to their server. In-server relocation uses Direct Relocate from the Alliance Shop, and joining an alliance below Sanctuary 8 teleports you next to the leader for free. Server time is UTC-2; Alliance Duel days reset at 00:00 UTC.
