## Setup

The Research Lab opens at Sanctuary 7. The 2nd Workbench at Sanctuary 9 gives you a second parallel queue. Both should be running around the clock. Alliance Help works on research.

Trees unlock by Lab level: Development and Economy at 1, Hero at 5, Soldier at 6, Squad trees at 8, **Alliance Duel at 10** (some sites say 15), Full Development and Prosperous Economy at 13, Caravan Transport at 15, Elite Troop at 20, Offensive and Defensive Tactics at 20 to 21, faction Masteries at 30.

## Cheapest Might

Resources per Might point: Development 794, Economy 2,640, Soldier 12,152, Hero 18,065. Development and Economy sell Might cheapest, which matters for Survival Battle and Alliance Duel "Tech Might" scoring.

Construction Master makes buildings **cheaper** (up to 5%), not faster.

## Recommended order

<div data-widget="research-order"></div>

1. **Development** (build and research speed, infirmary capacity, healing).
2. **Alliance Duel tree** as soon as you have Study Scrolls (below).
3. **Economy**, gathering branch first.
4. **Hero**, the faction of your Squad 1.
5. **Soldiers**: Load Training (troop capacity) first, then training speed and capacity, then ATK, DEF, HP.
6. **Squad 1**. Squad 1 and Squad 4 give the same bonuses as Squad 2 and 3 for a fraction of the cost.
7. **Elite Troop**: only the node you need for the next tier.
8. Squads 2 and 3, Tactics, Caravan Transport last.

Spenders with a permanent second queue: run Alliance Duel and Development in parallel.

## The Alliance Duel tree

Costs Study Scrolls only, 54,110 for the whole branch. Scrolls come from Alliance Duel results (420 a week for a loss, 840 for a win), Alliance League, the Lion Coin shop and event chests. A small alliance that loses every week takes a very long time here, which is one reason KMA needs everyone on their Duel day.

Buy in this order:

| Node | Scrolls | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Super Rewards | 100 | Unlocks Duel chest tiers 4 to 6 |
| Best Rewards | 450 | Unlocks Duel chest tiers 7 to 9 |
| Arena Expert | 3,800 | Doubles every point source in the Duel |
| Arena Master | later | +50% points |
| Everything else | remaining | Multiplies specific point sources |

**T10 troops need one specific node in the Elite Troop category, not the whole branch.** Maxing the branch costs about twice what access needs. The Caravan Transport tree (81,950 scrolls) competes for the same scrolls; leave it until T10 is done.

## Research speedups

Score 50 points per minute on Alliance Duel day 3 (and days 5 to 6). Study Scrolls score 300 each on day 3. Start long research on day 2 so it lands on day 3, and save research speedups for Wednesdays.
