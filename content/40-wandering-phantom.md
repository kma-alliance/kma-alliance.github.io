## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Needs | Sanctuary 8 |
| Spawns | Every 10 minutes, up to 70 on the map, each alive for 4 hours |
| Levels | 100 to 190, recommended Power 1.4M to 34M |
| First-kill reward | Claimable 3 times a day |

## Rewards

- 28 kingdom quests: first kills per level, kill counters (20 to 100), participation (50 to 500 Doctors).
- Damage ranking by mail: rank 1 gets 50 SR supplies of each type plus 5 general; ranks 51 to 200 get 10 plus 1.
- Per-level drops include Raven Fruit (1,000 to 1,600), Antitoxin (4k to 279k), a Recruit Ticket, SR Gear Supply, 2 Speedup Supplies and a Survivor Recruit Ticket.

Blights that hit a territory do not relocate it and kill fewer soldiers than a player attack, but you still defend, so keep a garrison set.

## KMA plan

Officers rally the high-level Phantoms; members solo the ones inside their Power. Hold the Raven Fruit for Duel day 1.
