The **Era of Revival** is the late-game phase that follows Survival and Growth. It arrives as a 56-day season, but the systems it unlocks stay with your account after the season ends. Two things decide how well you do: **starting the Lord Statue chain on day 1**, because Resistance compounds, and **putting your shards into one hero** instead of spreading them.

## How it works

Everything inside an Era counts from **Era day 1**, not from server age. A new Era is announced 5 days before it starts. Kingdoms 33 to 64 entered the Era of Revival on 13 July 2026, and servers in the Era close to new characters.

Some systems run for the whole season: the Era Covenant pass, Era bonuses, Lord Specialty, Resistance, the Weekly Pass and the [Revival Expedition League](#/kingdom-clash). The hero content arrives one window at a time.

## The Era Covenant pass

The Era Covenant is the season's battle pass. It has **50 levels**, and the last one opens at **35,000 points**. Points come from seven daily tasks worth 1,100 a day, plus 6,600 from one-time tasks. There is one free track and two paid tracks; a player's screenshots label the three tiers Basic, Epic and Mythic.

## Lord Statue, Specialty and Resistance

The **Lord Statue** costs almost nothing and gives no Power, but it opens **Lord's Specialty**: 40 levels, one skill point per level. The statue's slot appears at Sanctuary 10; both skill branches open at Sanctuary 14.

- **Handyman** is the economy branch: instant production, free build and research minutes, cheaper costs, help for allies, a shield.
- **Legion Commander** is the war branch: training speed, Barracks and Infirmary capacity, rally damage, reinforcement speed, emergency healing.

There are 183 skill levels in total and only 40 points, so you can take less than a quarter. Passive skills pay back best. You can reset, but only with a Specialty Reset Book.

Each Specialty level also produces **Resistance XP** every hour, from 1,080 at level 1 to 43,200 at level 40. Elite first clears and monster kills add more. Resistance works like an entry threshold: monsters need a minimum Resistance, and in fights the side with more Resistance gains an edge. It only helps **up to the enemy's value**, so catching up pays and overshooting does not.

The **Weekly Pass** lasts 7 days and adds Resistance +250 and March Speed +5% while active, plus 20,000 Specialty XP, 100 diamonds, a stamina item and 80 five-minute speedups each day.

## Awakening and Exclusive Weapons

**Hero Awakening** needs a **10-star** hero. Opening it costs 50 Awaken Shards, and all 40 levels cost 2,870 more. Every level adds damage resistance, from 1% at level 1 to 5% at level 40. Hero-specific shards come mostly from paid bundles. Free shards come from Alliance Duel, Era rewards, the Honor and Expedition shops and awakening chests from the Kingdom War season.

**Exclusive Weapons** belong to one hero each and cannot be moved. One player guide puts the unlock at 30 weapon shards.

> **Unverified:** the star count for Exclusive Weapons is not settled. This page said stars 1 to 7; a Russian walkthrough breaks down skill upgrades at 4, 6, 8 and 10 stars. Check the weapon screen before you plan shard purchases.

The **Soldier's Rest** building brings back part of your dead soldiers, on a cooldown and at a resource cost.

## The KMA plan

1. **Before the Era:** Sanctuary 30, Squad 1, Soldier and Elite Troop research done. Stockpile speedups, Antitoxin, medals, Arthur and Marlena shards and diamonds.
2. **Day 1:** build the Lord Statue and start Specialty. Resistance compounds, so every day you wait costs you.
3. **Every day:** the seven pass dailies, before anything optional.
4. **Priority:** main hero's Awakening, then that hero's Exclusive Weapon, then Specialty and Resistance, then Era bonuses, then secondary heroes.

> **KMA:** Handyman suits most members most of the season. Players who lead rallies in Kingdom War or the League get more from Legion Commander. Think before you spend points: a reset needs a Specialty Reset Book, and only the top paid track includes one.

## Quick answers

**Does the Era end after 56 days?** The season does. Awakening, weapons, Specialty and the other systems stay.

**When is my Era day 1?** Your Era's own start date, not your server's age. Watch for the announcement 5 days before.

**I missed a hero window. Can I still get it?** Not this Era. A missed window waits for the next one.

**Handyman or Legion Commander?** Handyman if you mostly build and research, Legion Commander if you fight and rally. You can reset, but only with a Specialty Reset Book.
