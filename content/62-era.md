## Era Covenant

The season's battle pass. Player videos and a player's own screenshots call it the **Era Covenant**; some guide sites call it the Era Pass. It is the same thing.

| | |
|---|---|
| Season | 56 days |
| Levels | 50, opening at 35,000 points |
| Points | 7 daily tasks = 1,100 a day, plus 40 one-time tasks = 6,600 |
| Finish | Around day 26 if you do the dailies |

| Reward at level 50 | Free | Pass | Large Pass |
|---|---|---|---|
| Recruit Tickets | 4 | 45 | 70 |
| UR Hero Omni Shards | 5 | 17 | 34 |
| 100-diamond packs | – | 5 | 39 |
| Lord's Specialty XP | 14,000 | 29,000 | 58,000 |
| 50-stamina packs | 1 | 5 | 10 |

Do the seven dailies. The one-time tasks are optional padding. Recruit tickets are the reason to buy a track, if you buy one.

## Era of Revival

The third phase after Survival and Growth. Live on Kingdoms 33 to 64 from 13 July 2026, rolling to servers older than about 130 days. Permanent endgame systems, not a 30-day event. Servers in the Era close to new characters.

| System | What it is |
|---|---|
| Hero Awakening | Needs a **10-star** hero first. Waves: Marlena first, then Annie and Cynthia, Arthur expected next. Each window lasts 7 days. Omni Awaken Shards come free from Alliance Duel and events; hero-specific shards from passes and daily offers |
| Exclusive Weapons | Hero-specific, stars 1 to 7 (Trials tracks 3 tasks per star). First for Daskal and Louis around week 4, Ulfrid week 6, Omni weapon shards weeks 7 to 8 |
| Revival Expedition League | Replaces Kingdom War: 16 kingdoms, seasonal, one strongest march per player. See [Kingdom Clash and Revival League](#/kingdom-clash) |
| Lord Specialty and Skills | Two branches: PvP (attack, march size, hero damage) or Economy (production, research, build, healing). Aim for Specialty 40 during the window; skills persist |
| Lord Resistance | Damage reduction, troop survivability, healing bonuses |
| Era Enhancement | City and hero skins gain stats. One skin at a time |

**Priority order:** main hero's Awakening → that hero's Exclusive Weapon → Lord Specialty and Resistance → Era Enhancement → secondary heroes → League play.

**Before your server enters:** Sanctuary 30, Squad 1, Soldier and Elite Troop research done, stockpiles of speedups, Antitoxin, medals, Arthur and Marlena shards, diamonds.

> **Avoid:** Awakening several heroes at once, putting weapons on bench heroes, panic spending in the first 72 hours, or buying an awakening pass without a 10-star hero.
