## Rewards

Every question pays **10 diamonds if right, 5 if wrong**, so a run pays 75 to 150 diamonds before milestones.

| Correct | Milestone reward |
|---|---|
| 1 | 10 Diamonds, 5 Resource Supply (SR), 3 each of SR Grain, Timber and Herb Level Supplies |
| 3 | 2 Survivor Recruit Tickets, 10 Resource Supply (SR), 5 of each Level Supply |
| 5 | 30 Diamonds, 20 Resource Supply (SR), 8 of each Level Supply |
| 7 | 3 Survivor Recruit Tickets, 30 Resource Supply (SR), 10 of each Level Supply |
| 10 | 5 Survivor Recruit Tickets, 60 Diamonds, 80 Resource Supply (SR) |

## Quick answers

**What if I get everything wrong?** You still get 75 diamonds.

**Do I need all 15 right?** No. Ten correct claims every milestone; the last five add 5 diamonds each.

**Can my alliance help?** Yes. You can ask alliance members for the answer, on top of 3 hints per run.

**Where do I find it?** In the Special Events tab on Sundays, from Sanctuary 6.
