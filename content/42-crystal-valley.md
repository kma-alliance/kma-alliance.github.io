Some menus and the wiki call this the **Black Mine**. It is the same event.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Needs | Sanctuary 15. First session in server week 3 |
| Format | 30-minute sessions, up to 350 players per map matched by Sanctuary tier (15 to 25, up to 37 to 45) |
| Troops | Provided by the event, they do not die. Territories are safe |
| Relocation | Free every 6 minutes |

## The map

| Vein | Stock | Per unit |
|---|---|---|
| Cluster 1 | 8,640 | 12 |
| Cluster 2 | 17,280 | 24 |
| Cluster 3 | 34,560 | 48 |
| Instant Cluster 4 | 4,000 | 200 |

Instant mines spawn at minutes **10, 11, 12, 20, 21 and 22**. The **Sacred Cave** opens at minute 23. Gathering points, even allies', can be seized; a failed seizure stacks a hero debuff on you.

## Mine skills

Ten skills in five pairs, costing 200, 500, 900, 1,500 and 2,300 crystals. The ones that matter: Plague Rat Hunter (+1,500 crystals per rat), Valley Plunder (+5,000 on a seizure), Crystal Resonance (+50% gathering for 60 seconds).

## Rewards

Personal milestones up to 150,000 crystals pay 2,000 Fame Medals, 100 diamonds, 2 UR Omni Shards and 50 Tempered Steel. Rank 1 gets 2 UR Curio Chests, 15 UR Omni Shards and 30 UR supplies of each type. Fort rankings at 100k, 80k, 60k; zone standings at 300k, 250k, 210k.

## KMA plan

Start on a Cluster 3, buy Crystal Resonance early, be next to an instant mine at minute 10 and 20, and hunt Plague Rats between them. Seize only what you can hold.
